Cryptocurrencies are purchased on [**Exchanges**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/quale-exchange/): such as Binance, Kraken, Coinbase or Crypto.com. After purchasing them, you can choose where to leave them: either on the Exchange's wallet or on a **Wallet**.

An **offline Wallet** is a place to keep your [Cryptocurrencies](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/criptovalute-sono-il-futuro/) safe: this means that all information will be **private** and inaccessible, precisely because they work offline.

These wallets have a security key (**recovery seed**) that only you will know, but be careful, in case of loss of the security phrase you will never be able to access your digital currencies again.

There are no **password recovery** procedures. It will be composed of English words that make sense, but are not logically connected to each other.

This method of holding will not allow any **hacker** to access them, but places a greater **responsibility** on you.

## Types of Wallets

There are two main types of **Off-line Wallets** where you can safely store your Cryptocurrencies.

* **Virtual** Wallets
* **Physical** Wallets (Hard Wallet)

Offline Wallets are also called **Cold Wallets**. I wrote a guide about it: [Cold Wallet: Everything you need to know](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/cold-wallet/)

## What is a Virtual Wallet?

Virtual Wallets are **software** that you can download on your computer and work offline unlike Hot Wallets.

In this way, you can hold Crypto on your computer in total **security**.

## What is a Hard Wallet?

A **Hard Wallet**, or physical Wallet, is a **tangible wallet**, which instead of being a software to download can be a **USB stick** or similar.



In addition to being considered even more secure, it can be much more convenient as you can carry or hide Cryptocurrencies more easily. It can then be connected to the computer via USB stick or to the phone with an application.

## Cryptocurrency Security

Unfortunately, Cryptocurrencies have been **stolen** several times from Exchanges through Hacker attacks, so, despite the exchanges mentioned above and many others being renowned and widely used, you will never have complete certainty that this cannot happen.

This is the main reason why many choose a Hard Wallet. From the security point of view, there are no comparisons, the physical wallet represents the **best choice**.

## MY Cryptocurrencies

Possessing Crypto on **Exchanges** is against the concept of **Blockchain** itself, which promotes **total decentralization**.

Being an exchange a centralized platform, holding Crypto there would be like investing in it but **not really owning them**.

This is because they actually remain in the hands of the **broker**, as they custody your private keys, not you.

## Cryptocurrency transfer speed

Although this is an article mainly reserved for Holders, we must also consider the speed factor.

Cryptos held in a Hard Wallet will be less accessible and the process to bring them back to an exchange to sell or send them is not immediate.

If they are held on a broker the process will be **instantaneous**: you can send or sell immediately.

There can always be **emergencies or unforeseen circumstances** and from this point of view Exchanges surpass Hard Wallets.
