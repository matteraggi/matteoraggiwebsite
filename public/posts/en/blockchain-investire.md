## Blockchain investing: The best methods

**Investing in Blockchain** is not as simple as one might think.

It is a **technology** that is now already used and supported by many companies, but **investing** in them does not ensure a good return.

It is not said that just because a **company** uses **Blockchain** it will be successful in the future.

The company could still **fail**.

Same thing with **cryptocurrencies**.

They are based on **Blockchain** but not all will gain value over time. Indeed, few will do so in the **long term**.

So it is complicated to achieve "pure" exposure to **Blockchain** in our portfolio, without increasing the risk too much.

Let's evaluate the pros and cons of all the options we have.

### Investing in Crypto

**Investing in Crypto** is certainly the most direct method.

The correlation of a **Cryptocurrency** with its underlying **Blockchain** is maximum. The very value of the currency will depend on what its **Blockchain** is capable of doing.

It requires a good knowledge of **Crypto**. To be able to choose the right **Blockchain** to invest in, you need to study it thoroughly and understand if it can find its space on the **market**.

To better understand the validity of a Blockchain it can be useful to study the Blockchain Trilemma, on which I wrote an article: [What is the Blockchain Trilemma? The 6 best solutions](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/trilemma-blockchain/).

It will not be an investment on **Blockchain Technology** but rather on one or a few single Blockchains.

And this perhaps is not what you want to achieve.

What is certain is that if the **Blockchain** is successful, its **token** will also rise. It is the method with the highest correlation of all.

### Which Crypto to buy?

I am not a **financial advisor** so I will not propose specific **Cryptocurrencies** that you can buy.

To choose the right **Cryptocurrency** you will have to analyze its **Blockchain**.

If you intend to buy one or at most two, I can advise you not to raise the risk too much and bet on already known **Crypto**: **Bitcoin** and **Ethereum**.

### Cryptocurrency Fund

A **cryptocurrency fund** can be a good option if your main fear is the high **volatility** of **Crypto** but you still seek a high correlation.

A **basket of Crypto** is certainly less volatile than a single **token**. Having more **Cryptocurrencies** allows you to be protected in case a project collapses to zero.

Which is not very rare in this world not yet properly regulated.

So another point against the previous method is solved: the **investment** will be more **distributed**.

Volatility remains very high anyway being a pure **ETF** on **cryptocurrencies**. This means you will hold **tokens**. Even if **distributed** they will be more **volatile** than stocks on the **stock market**.

### How to compose a Cryptocurrency fund?

There are different schools of thought for the structure of a **cryptocurrency fund**.

All share a very important factor: the predominance of **Bitcoin**.

Bitcoin is very **volatile**, but despite this, it is the least volatile **Cryptocurrency** of all.

Furthermore, a large part of the **tokens** on the market follow the exact same movement as **Bitcoin**, only more accentuated.

Consequently, I keep at least **75%** of my [portfolio](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/metamask/) in Bitcoin.

The remaining **25%** is divided into 20% **Ethereum** and 5% maximum on other higher risk **Crypto** (still remaining among the most **capitalized**).

### Token Presale

The **Token presale** is another method based directly on the **purchase** of **cryptocurrencies**.

In this case, instead of buying **Crypto** already released on the market, you invest in **tokens** that have yet to come out.

It is even more complicated than the methods already seen. It can give great satisfaction, but it certainly requires prior knowledge.

You can buy **projects** at a very low price, which therefore allows you to have a good profit **margin**.

Finding the right project among all those that continuously come out is very complicated and the **investment risk** increases.

Many of the **Cryptocurrencies** that are created fail and disappear shortly after. Almost all after a **Bear Market**.

For this reason, if you have never operated on **Cryptocurrencies**, it is an option that I strongly advise against.

### Blockchain investing in stocks

**Investment** in **stocks** is suitable for two types of people:

- those who do not want a **high risk** portfolio
- those who do not have an account on an [**Exchange**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/quale-exchange/) and do not want to create one

Stocks are on average less volatile than **Cryptocurrencies**. And not everyone has an account on an **Exchange**.

Without it, you cannot **buy Cryptocurrencies**, or in any case, it is quite difficult.

If you don't want to create a separate **account** just to invest in Cryptocurrencies, this method is for you. You can invest in **Blockchain** also with **stocks** of companies that deal with that.

Although they are not yet big companies, there are some already established realities.

There are two types of stocks you can invest in:

- **blockchain** companies
- companies with **blockchain interests**

The former are companies that deal with **Blockchain services**, so they work mainly with this technology, like [**Coinbase**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/quale-exchange/).

The latter are companies that deal with something else but have shown **interest in investing** in Blockchain or are opening dedicated branches, such as **Paypal**.

**Investing** in the former allows you to have a **greater correlation**, but they are probably smaller companies, therefore **riskier**.

On the contrary, the latter can be larger companies but the **correlation** can also be **minimal**. Which is not necessarily bad, it depends on your goal.

### Blockchain investing in crowdfunding

**Crowdfunding** is perhaps the method one thinks of least when talking about **investments**.
