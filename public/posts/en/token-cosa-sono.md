## But what are Tokens?

**Token** is a particular word. One might think it has a specific meaning, when the reality is different.

In fact, Tokens are simply "**digital assets**" based on the Blockchain.

But wait, stay with me, I'll explain better now.

Do you know what a Blockchain is?

I'll explain it very quickly.

It is a decentralized network to which a **Cryptocurrency** is associated that contributes to its sustenance, useful for rewarding the workers who keep it running.

Many things can be done on this network, in fact, it is a **decentralized** database (set of data). Wherever data needs to be stored, Blockchain can be applied.

The most known are those of **Bitcoin**, Ethereum, and Solana.

With their associated Cryptos. But it doesn't end here, because Tokens come into play.

In fact, plot twist, Cryptocurrencies are **Tokens**.

And they are the most "important" of the network, as they guarantee its functioning.

However, alternative Tokens can be created on the same network. And usually, the term "token" refers precisely to these.

They are therefore, as I mentioned before, digital assets based on the **Blockchain**. And we are talking about assets of any kind.

Let me give you an example.

On the **Ethereum** network, in addition to the proprietary Cryptocurrency of the same name, numerous tokens have been created.

- **NFT**

The **NFTs** we know, those for sale on [Opensea](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/opensea-come-funziona/), are Tokens based on the Ethereum network.

- **Decentraland**

[Decentraland](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/decentraland/) is a **Gaming** platform based on the Ethereum network. The game currency is a token. Same thing goes for [The Sandbox](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/the-sandbox/).

- **Curve**

**Curve** is a decentralized finance platform that offers an efficient way to exchange Cryptocurrencies (especially stablecoins) with low costs. Their proprietary Token, Curve, is based on the Ethereum network.

Since the Ethereum network is already very expanded and rich in **applications**, I could go on forever.

Furthermore, many other things can be done with Tokens on Blockchain that are still little explored. Like for example **Real Estate Tokenization**.

## There are 7 types of Tokens + Bonus

You didn't expect so many types of **Tokens** to exist, did you?

Instead, there probably exist even more than the ones I will tell you about today.

This is thanks to the fact that **Blockchain** application development is in its golden moment, and it is also very rapid.

Ideas and projects related to this world are born continuously, and it is complicated to keep up.

But it will certainly take a few years before seeing some valid idea take hold.

### Payment Coins

Payment Coins are Tokens used for **payment**. So all the main Cryptocurrencies ([layer 1](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/layer-1-crypto/)) you know are part of it.

However, other **Tokens** are also part of it. The purpose, as can be easily understood, is to be used as an alternative form of payment.

Even though most of them were born before the explosion of **decentralized finance**, therefore before Ethereum's success.

### Security Tokens

Security Tokens are a type of Token dangerous to handle lately.

This is because recently the **SEC**, Security Exchange Commission, has declared many Cryptocurrencies as such.

And the main problem is that in America there is still no **regulation** for this type of Token, consequently, they could not be traded.

However, the matter is not yet concluded, so at the moment they are all still present on major **Exchanges**. And furthermore, it must be considered that these are laws valid only for America.

So what are these Tokens?

Security Tokens represent fractional ownership of an **Asset**.

So they can be a house, works of art, or above all, they can represent **shares** of a company.

The Token can therefore represent to all intents and purposes a part of ownership of it, having also a correlated **trend**.

And this is the main problem that **Security Tokens** have with the American government at the moment.

Even Tokens like **BNB** (Binance's proprietary Token) have been accused of being one.

Here a very long parenthesis could be opened about it. Because surely BNB can be considered a Security Token but it is not only that.

In fact, BNBs are also, and above all, **Utility Tokens**. So they can effectively be used for the functioning of various parts of the platform.

Let's deepen Utility Tokens better.

### Utility Tokens

Utility Tokens are certainly the most interesting. And lately, they are spreading a lot.

They are in fact **Tokens** that serve and are used for the functioning of a service or product.

The most striking example is **Ethereum**. The Token is the fuel of the platform. And it is thanks to it that a decentralized network as wide as Ethereum manages to work.

Other Tokens of this type are: BNB, CRO (Crypto.com Token), or YNG (Young Platform Token).

But in the case of Utility Tokens of [Exchanges](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/migliori-exchange-crypto/) like the previous ones, be careful. They could in fact also be recognized as Security Tokens.

### Governance Tokens

These are Tokens that are used to take part in a project. So for example to **vote** on decisions to be made.

Used obviously in **decentralized finance** projects.

Some examples are: UNI (Token of the Uniswap platform) and MANA (Token of the Gaming platform **Decentraland**).

### NFT

We all know **NFTs** by now. Well yes, they are also tokens. A bit different though.

In fact, as the name says, they are **Non-Fungible Tokens**. So Tokens that are not fungible, not exchangeable one-for-one.

Unique Tokens, with a standalone value.

Some NFT collections have then associated a fungible Token with it. For example, the **ApeCoin** Token of the renowned Bored Apes collection.

Attention. This is not an invitation to **trade** NFTs. Expecting a return from such an activity is madness.

It involves only luck and exploiting a trend. So remember that this is an informative guide.

### Storage Tokens

These are Tokens that deal with **Cloud Storage**. Even if it seems like a very particular niche, there are Tokens that are part of it.

They are therefore platforms similar to **iCloud**, Google Photos, Amazon Photos, but decentralized.

They actually have great positive sides, such as:

- more security
- lower costs
- more transparency
- faster network

The main Cryptocurrencies of this type are **Filecoin** and **BitTorrent**.

## FAQ

**What is a token?**

A Token is a digital asset based on a Blockchain network.

**How much is a token worth in euros?**

There is no single answer to this question. There are different types of Tokens with different values.

**What is the difference between a token and a cryptocurrency?**

A Cryptocurrency owns a Blockchain and contributes to its functioning. A token instead does not have its own Blockchain but relies on an existing one.

**What does it mean to pay in tokens?**

It means paying using a digital currency that relies on a Blockchain network that guarantees functioning and security.

**How many types of tokens are there?**

There are many different types of Tokens. We could classify at least 8 main ones. Some of these are: Utility, Security, Governance,…

**What can I do with tokens?**

Based on the type of Token we can perform different actions. There are Payment Coins with which you can pay, Governance Tokens with which you can take part in a decision-making process of a project, and there are also Utility Tokens that allow performing specific functions based on the platform. Then many others exist.

**Which tokens to buy?**

I cannot recommend which Token to buy, everyone must do their own research. Despite this, I advise against buying little-known tokens, as they certainly have a more volatile trend.

**Where are tokens purchased?**

Tokens can be bought in two places: either Classic Exchanges, where you can find the most known Tokens, or Decentralized Exchanges, where you can also find the less capitalized ones.

**How is a token generated?**

It is very simple to create a token and it can be done on different networks. The best one to do it on is the Ethereum network, thanks to which you can create ERC-20 tokens.
Just go to an ERC-20 Token generation site and fill in the required data.
