## Staking

**Staking** is one of the most used methods to earn a small percentage passively and safely from your **investments**.

The procedure is simple. Do you have Cryptocurrencies? You can **lock** them for a certain (or indeterminate) period of time in exchange for an extra percentage.

And it is an extremely wide world. There is a sea of opportunities.

What do I mean by this?

I mean that Staking **rewards** can vary enormously. Based on these factors:

- Capital in Staking (obviously)
- Staking Risk
- Staking Duration

You can decide the capital in staking up to a certain point: it depends on how many **Crypto** you own.

If you do not intend to sell any of your Cryptos you can think of locking a good part of them. However, this also depends on the **staking risk**.

Staking Risk depends on two factors:

- The **Cryptocurrency** you lock
- The **Platform** you use

Locking [Bitcoin](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/investire-in-bitcoin/) is different from locking Cardano, ChainLink, or Solana. The more the value of a Crypto fluctuates, the higher the Staking **yield** will be. **StableCoins** can also be locked.

But a big difference is made by the **platform**. And here there are several paths that can be taken. Because there are two major types of platforms offering this service:

- **Centralized** platforms (more security, lower yields)
- **Decentralized** platforms (less security, higher yields)

As always, the equation applies: More risks = More (possible) yields.

The important thing is to choose platforms that are not a scam, pay close attention to that.

I would pay particular attention to the **reward lock-up** period. I would prefer services that allow withdrawing rewards immediately.

Some of the most famous platforms offering the possibility of staking are:

- [Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/it) (Centralized Finance)
- [Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/activity/referral-entry/CPA?ref=CPA_00UL1P5UZR) (DeFi and CeFi)
- [PancakeSwap](https://pancakeswap.finance) (DeFi)
- ...

**Staking Duration** is totally subjective. It is usually done by those who do not intend to sell the Cryptocurrency for at least a few months.

But this also depends on which **Crypto** you are putting in Staking. Bitcoin as far as I'm concerned can be left for several months, while other **Altcoins** are certainly riskier to do so.

In some cases, you can even choose the option that allows you to interrupt the lock when you prefer.

The longer the period of time for which you choose to lock the currency, the higher the **percentage yield** will be.

The **Liquidity Pool** is actually a method similar to Staking. Together with the previous one, they are the methods that can have a higher return.

Earning with them means providing liquidity to this pool, in such a way as to allow faster [Trading](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/guida-trading-base/).

You get rewarded for the liquidity provided with interest.

This is very useful. Because a **thin market** is an unhealthy market. And one in which no one wants to find themselves.

## Airdrop

**Crypto AirDrops**. Complicated topic this one.

Why?

Well, it certainly offers earning possibilities less secure than other methods. In fact, it is not certain that one can make a **profit**.

So I advise you to associate it with other methods, not use it alone.

But I still haven't told you what it is.

An AirDrop is a **marketing** strategy that is carried out by Crypto projects yet to launch on the market.

To start the project, **Cryptocurrencies** are distributed for free, through an Airdrop (a drop, a release) indeed.

At first, these **tokens** will not have particular value, but by participating in multiple AirDrops you could be lucky.

Unfortunately, not everything is so simple.

In fact, as you may have guessed, not everyone can **participate in AirDrops**.

Launches of larger projects have accurate **selection**. So to have a chance to receive their tokens for free it might be necessary to perform **on-chain** actions like swapping or providing liquidity.

Only smaller projects might be without particular requirements.

## Bonus

**Bonuses** can be exploited sometimes, but they do not represent a **constant** income. Similarly to AirDrops, they can generate a gain only occasionally.

I'm talking about **Welcome Bonuses** or some special limited-time Bonus.

These Bonuses can often also be **considerable**, but as you well know they can be exploited only once.

So, it is certainly worth considering them, but it is still a less efficient technique than Staking or Liquidity Pool.
