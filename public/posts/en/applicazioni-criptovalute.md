### Binance

**Binance** is one of the most used Exchanges. I talked in depth about the platform in [this article](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/migliori-exchange-crypto/).

Its **Mobile Application** is the best regarding adaptability. What does it mean?

You must know that it is structured in two modes: the Lite version and the Pro version.

But these are not two different **applications**, you can go from one mode to another whenever you prefer.

They are both very useful. And above all, it is configured as a perfect solution for those who have just started but intend to deepen in the future.

The Lite part is very simple to use. However, it only has basic functions.

While the Pro version is a bit more confusing to understand but through it, it is possible to do anything imaginable in the **Crypto** world.

Binance is indeed one of the most complete platforms from this point of view. And it also has reduced costs.

I recommend it to anyone and it is the **platform** I certainly use the most.

### Crypto.com

[Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/it) is a platform existing only as a Mobile Application. Consequently, ease of use is excellent.

All functions are in their place and it is very simple to navigate on the **platform**.

It has more functions than Binance Lite and is still easy to use, so if you are not interested in exploiting everything Binance Pro has to offer, Crypto.com is definitely better.

In fact, the extra functions that Binance Pro has are not fundamental.

They can certainly be useful and handy, but they are not essential.

**Crypto.com** instead already has good **Staking** functions for example, but without giving up the simplicity of the platform.

It also has a **debit card** with not bad Bonuses.

Binance also has its own card, but it returns only a very small percentage of cashback.

While the **Crypto.com card** is more interesting. It is present in several levels, which are unlocked by staking (=locking, without the possibility of selling them) a certain amount of their Crypto, **CRO**.

I won't report the figures here because unfortunately they change often. When I took the Red card years ago I had to stake €350.

Bonuses can be: Spotify refund, Netflix refund, Lounge access. And cashback that increases with the level.

The red card I own includes the **Spotify** refund.

[**Open a Crypto.com Account**](https://crypto.com/it)

### Kraken

[Kraken](https://www.kraken.com) is one of the first **Exchanges** ever created. It is considered among the safest.

It also has a structure similar to Binance with two **functions**: Standard and Pro.

However, Kraken has divided the two functions into two different **applications**, downloadable separately.

It is certainly more uncomfortable than the Binance solution, but not a big deal.

The simple **App** is very similar to Crypto.com in terms of functionality.

However, it must be considered that Crypto.com does not give the possibility, in any case, to access more complex functions.

Which are instead accessible from the **Website** or the Pro app in the case of Kraken.

So, summarizing the 3 Exchanges:

- **Binance**

App with two modes: either very simple or with complex features. And Website with the same features as the Pro version App.

- **Crypto.com**

Only Mobile Application, with all necessary basic functions and very simple to use.

- **Kraken**

Standard App similar to Crypto.com: simple and with all basic features. Pro App similar to Binance Pro. Website rich in features, similar to Binance Exchange (although Binance has extra functions).
