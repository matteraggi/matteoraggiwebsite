## What NFTs really are

There is only one way to better understand what **NFTs** really are.

And that is digging deep, beyond the applications that already exist today.

This is because for the moment there are few use cases.

Let's start with their name: NFT. It stands for **Non-Fungible Token**.

Non-fungible means not interchangeable. In fact, every NFT is unique.

Examples of fungible (interchangeable) Tokens are instead **Cryptocurrencies**. So 1 Bitcoin can be exchanged for 1 Bitcoin, while an NFT can never be exchanged for another NFT.

When you think of an NFT you should therefore think of the technology behind it, always based on [Blockchain](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/trilemma-blockchain/), like **Crypto**.

The common characteristics of all NFTs are the following:

- **Digital** objects
- **Not interchangeable** with each other
- Certified on **Blockchain**

But what do monkeys have to do with it?

I just explained the technology of NFTs. Applying it in different fields can yield different results.

For example, when we apply the technology to digital photos we obtain the result known to everyone.

**Digital images**, not interchangeable with each other and certified. These three characteristics put together allow sale and collecting.

In fact, we could always recognize the original.

So the digital monkeys we all know are just an application of this technology, but they do not represent it.

Well, now that you have understood what **NFTs** really are, we can hypothesize future applications.

### Digital Identification

In this era where almost everything is moving online, **documents** still cannot do so.

One of the biggest problems of digital is the ease of forgery.

Like banknotes, identification documents have complex particularities to emulate. They are very difficult to falsify.

With **digital documents** it is different.

In fact, they can be tampered with in less than 5 minutes. With very few moves and simple requirements.

Just knowing how to use an application like **Photoshop** and we can change images and dates on any document.

This is the reason why a photo of one's document in many cases is not sufficient, the physical copy is needed. But there is a way to create a valid digital document.

Through a state-managed **NFT** system, the validity of a document could be recognized even if **not physical**.

Basing one's digital identity on a **Blockchain** platform, it would be impossible to modify such data.

A simple and efficient system. Even safer than physical documentation.

### Music streaming

Musicians are not doing so well with streaming on Spotify or Apple Music.

I know, I know, you might be thinking: "There are people worse off, it doesn't seem like someone like **Sfera Ebbasta** has money problems".

But I'm not talking about them, international Stars.

They manage to earn tens of thousands of Euros for every Live performance. And it is thanks to them that they find themselves in that specific **financial position**.

I am talking about all the musicians in the world. Not so well known as to be able to earn well thanks to their skills.

Even with a few hundred thousand monthly Streamings on **digital platforms**, you can't earn a living.

In fact, according to the most recent data I managed to find, 100,000 listens are equivalent to about 400 dollars.

Unfeasible for a small artist.

Yet the reality of those who want to live off music is this. Not the one we see more often of popular Stars.

The few possibilities of **earning** discourage careers in this field, and induce treating music more like a hobby than a possible job.

Because effectively the percentage of artists with more than 400,000 monthly streamings on digital platforms (which would be the equivalent of about €1600 per month) is really low.

The solution?

Eliminate the **commissions** of intermediaries. In fact, artists cannot be truly independent.

To earn they must pass through record label commissions and **streaming platforms**.

Putting one's music at the service of everyone through an **NFT** system could bring numerous advantages.

Both with streaming and with purchase, a system could be built that exploits Non-Fungible Tokens to generate a **direct reward** and automatic to the artist.

Every stream or every purchase.

And this is possible thanks to the automatic "**Royalties**" system that can be exploited with NFTs.

Basically, the system based on **Blockchain** automatically rewards the creator of the work every time someone performs a certain action.

### Real estate trading

The trading of **large physical assets**. It might seem strange, but think about it carefully.

Large physical assets like buying a property require a lot of bureaucracy, many documents, and therefore a lot of time.

The "tokenization" (term indicating the representation in the form of a digital token) of a property could greatly speed up the process.

I'll give you a practical example.

Steps to buy a house today:

- Rely on an agency
- Verify that there is no hidden or falsified information (undeclared problems for example)
- Long negotiation
- Proposal and preliminary contract
- Important clauses to insert
- Closing the deed

As you can notice even without reading every step, it is a very long and tiring procedure.

The **technology** on which **NFTs** are based (Blockchain) can speed up the whole process (and by a lot).

For example:

- No hidden or falsified information (data protected by Blockchain)
- Online Auction Negotiation
- Automatic contract through [Smart Contract](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/terminologia-crypto/)
- No Deed, automatic change of ownership on Blockchain

All this happens thanks to Tokenization. What will be exchanged in ownership will be in fact the **identifying token** of the property.

It will replace the contract.

### Food Supply Chain

Maybe it is one of the most useful applications.

Already exploited by some supermarkets for internal uses, it is useful to know the **precise origin** of the product and all its ingredients.

Some innovative ideas concern the use through a simple **QR code**.
By scanning it anyone can verify the various origins.

And this can actually be applied even outside the food chain.

It can be applied to any sufficiently long **industrial chain**.

It can indeed be useful to:

- verify the veracity of information used for **marketing** purposes
- check the **quality** of a product

So for example it can also be applied to the clothing industrial chain.

### Video Games

In recent decades **video games** have increased their relevance year by year.

Both cultural and **economic**.

In fact, the video game industry currently exceeds (as **turnover**) the film and music industry combined.

The importance of this sector nowadays cannot be denied. And, given the **trend** of recent years, it is also difficult to bet against its continuation.

Even industries like **Meta** (new name of the Facebook company), which have always dealt with other things, are also moving in this direction with the Metaverse.

How will **NFTs** be important for video games?

It is actually the most intuitive application of all.

Already today millions of **digital items** are sold, whether they are cosmetics or have a particular utility.

Transforming them into NFTs would not change their nature at all. However, it would allow them to acquire the following capabilities:

- exchange between players
- buying and selling between players
- real value

Which could bring this world to an even greater **expansion**.

I believe it is only a matter of time before seeing large **video game companies** propose video games of this type.
