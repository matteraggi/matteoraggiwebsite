Where to get information about **Cryptocurrencies**?

Surely one of the main sources for a new and dynamic topic like Cryptocurrencies is Youtube.

Or information found on the Web.

But if you want to deepen the subject properly, you know: you have to read **books**.

And finding valid books on **Crypto** is not easy.

Stock market **investments** were born hundreds of years ago. The discipline has evolved a lot and there are various valid books about it.

The Intelligent Investor, Money Master the Game, One Up On Wall Street just to name a few.

**Cryptocurrencies**, on the other hand, have existed for less than 20 years. It is normal that there is less documentation on the matter.

Then if we add all the books written in 10 minutes just to exploit this **trend**, it is even more difficult to find a good product.

In fact, just go to Amazon and search for "Cryptocurrency Books" to realize that there are hundreds of poor-quality products, written with a Google search and also poorly translated.

But valid **Cryptocurrency books** do exist. And today I am here to recommend some to you. Starting with a great classic.

To know what I am referring to, you must read the next paragraph!

## You absolutely must read this book.

What am I talking about? Maybe you already know.

It is the most famous and renowned book in the Crypto field. The one that cannot be missed. The starting point for anyone who wants to understand **Bitcoin**.

It is **The Bitcoin Standard**.

A necessary Guide to understand what Bitcoin is and why it could be important in the future.

There is little to say about this book. It should be recommended to anyone who discredits Bitcoin without really knowing it. That is, to everyone, basically.

It manages very simply to explain how the **economy** has always worked, and how Bitcoin can fix many problems that man has created throughout his existence.

What the author shows is not an imaginary picture of what Bitcoin could be, exalted by his passion for the **Cryptocurrency**.

It is rather a realistic analysis based on data, information, and historical events on what **Bitcoin** could fix or improve in society.

If you haven't read it yet, you must remedy it immediately. It is the "**Bitcoin Bible**" and contains the basics to understand what can be done in the future with it.

## Continue reading with...

Besides "The Bitcoin Standard" there are other valid books. Let's see some!

### Investire in Bitcoin e Criptovalute - Luca Boiardi

Book written by [Luca Boiardi](https://www.youtube.com/@thecryptogateway), a disseminator in the field of Cryptocurrencies and DeFi on **YouTube**.

It is a book that manages to explain very well and simply what Cryptocurrencies are and what role they have played up to this moment.

It then concentrates on the Trading and **Investments** side, unlike The Bitcoin Standard which does not touch those topics.

I believe it is very easy to "get dirty" talking about topics of this type.

I, therefore, appreciate that The Bitcoin Standard does not talk about it, mostly for the role it has in the world of **Cryptocurrencies**. That of "Bible" or "Eternal Book".

I believe instead that Luca did very well to talk about Investments adequately in his book.

Always doing so taking into account the associated **risks**. Definitely one of the best crypto books.

### Mastering Bitcoin

This is definitely a more **technical** book than the previous ones, and not suitable for beginners.

It talks about Bitcoin from a still different point of view.

It tells it from the point of view of **programming**. It is indeed a fairly niche book.

I do not deny that it can be interesting even for those who do not know how to program, but it remains a very technical book and useful especially to an audience of programmers.

However, if you are interested in **Blockchain programming** in general there are better books, such as:

- [Mastering Blockchain](https://amzn.to/43AOhfu)
- [Blockchain Programming for Python Developers](https://amzn.to/3ORQsap)
- [Blockchain Developers Guide](https://amzn.to/3OSHytg)

These are books that I have not read though, so don't trust completely.

### Blockchain: Tecnologia e Applicazioni per il Business

This is an **informative** book, therefore not technical like the previous one, which however focuses on **Blockchain** instead of Bitcoin or Cryptocurrencies.

It is truly an excellent book for anyone who does not know much about Blockchain and wants to learn more.

Book accompanied by **illustrations** that help understanding and written in a clear and always understandable way.

There are also concepts on protocols, uses, and **economic** aspects.
