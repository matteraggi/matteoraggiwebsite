## Bitget review: Features and Services

The **BitGet** site is full of interesting features, but just like on **Binance**, these features can be confusing if not known well.

Let's explore them one by one, trying to understand which ones are the most interesting and which ones are not for us.

### "Buy Crypto" Section

The "**Buy Crypto**" section is dedicated to those who want to buy cryptocurrencies quickly, but **without trading**.

Without charts and without too many complications, it allows you to buy any **Cryptocurrency** very easily.

It has four features:

- **Express**

To buy or sell cryptocurrencies by simply setting the amount.

For those who want to understand each function even better, **BitGet** has created video guides that can be found at the top right once the mode to learn is chosen.

- **P2P Trading**

A feature that allows you to buy **Cryptocurrencies** directly from a user, instead of through the **Exchange**. A mode that I do not recommend you use because, at least in my opinion, it makes no particular sense.

- **Third-Party Payment**

Allows buying **Cryptocurrencies** through third-party payments.

- **Bank Deposit**

To deposit a certain amount on the **Exchange** that you can then convert into Cryptocurrencies.

### Trade Section

Section, as the name says, dedicated to **Trading** and all its options.

Entering this screen we will have the possibility to consult a chart and operate on the spot.

But there are different **Trading** options.

- **Futures Trading**

Thanks to this mode we could operate through **Futures**. A very delicate and complex trading topic, if you are interested I recommend you read my dedicated guide: [What are Crypto Futures: Cryptocurrency Trading](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/cosa-sono-i-futures-crypto/).

I do not recommend you operate with Futures if you are a beginner, it is a much more dynamic and articulated **Trading** mode.

- **Spot Trading**

The classic **market trading** mode. Perfect for anyone, especially for those who want to start.

To be used for medium-long term trades, while Futures especially for **day trading**.

- **Grid Trading**

This particular Trading mode operates using **Bots**.

I would not recommend you use it, except with a tiny part of the portfolio to try.

The **Grid Trading** mode is used especially for very marked trends or for strong sideways movement.

This is because bots are able to sell and buy quickly, realizing many micro-profits.

They do it through grids indeed: they sell once a high grid line is reached and buy once a low level is reached.

Keep in mind that even if the **Trading** mode is **automatic** you will have to set it up first. There is the possibility of using an **artificial intelligence** but I do not recommend you use it for the moment.

Artificial Intelligences have not yet reached a level to have good constant performance.

It can be used both Spot and Futures.

- **Spot Margin Trading**

It consists of **Spot Trading** but using loans to afford a **margin**.

I do not recommend you use this technique as it greatly increases the **risk** of trades.

- **MegaSwap**

Allows exchanging Cryptocurrencies with each other at the current rate. Instead of doing a double trade like **ETH-USDT** and **USDT-BTC** you can directly exchange **Ethereum** and **Bitcoin**. This allows saving on fees.

### Earn Section

In this section, there are **Extra** methods to profit from **Cryptocurrencies**.

- **Earn and Savings**

Section where with **Cryptocurrency** **Staking** you can receive a good annual return. For example, with **Stablecoin**, it is equal to 8%.

- **Shark fin**

Shark fin allows you, with a guaranteed capital system, to earn decent percentages in periods of about 7 days.

For example, with **Bitcoin**, it can go from 1 to 3%.

- **Dual investment**

Dual Investment is a type of **Trade** with a fixed action period.

Once the trade is chosen you can no longer stop it and the **Crypto** will be locked.

It is a method to increase the risk of a medium-long term investment.

- **Launchpool**

The Launchpool is the classic place where you can obtain **cryptocurrencies** that are to be launched by holding some capital in **staking** for a usually fixed period.

If you want to experiment and have some fun I recommend you check it out.

If you do not intend to sell your **cryptocurrencies** you can put some here, the risk is zero.

- **Earn BGB**

This is an area similar to "Earn and Savings" but only with **BGB** which is the **Exchange** Cryptocurrency.

Obviously being their **cryptocurrency** it has more convenient rates compared to others, but be careful.

It does not always mean that if the Exchange is valid their **Token** will rise, look for example at the trend of **CRO**.

- **BGB**

Presentation page of their Crypto.

- **Launchpad**

Same as Launchpool but instead of obtaining **tokens** for free through **staking** you will have to actually invest.

Obviously, the token being in the launch phase will have a favorable price.
