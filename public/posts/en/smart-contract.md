## What are Smart Contracts

Nowadays everything is Smart: Smart TV, Smart Home Speaker, Smart Lamp, Smart Plug, Smart Fridge and so on.

And in all these examples the word "Smart" stands for "object that can connect with other Smart objects and that is controllable remotely with a Smartphone".

But the "Smart" of Smart Contract is different. It has a totally different definition.

So what are Smart Contracts? Let's start with the definition of definitions: that of Wikipedia.

*Smart contracts are computer protocols that facilitate, verify, or enforce the negotiation or performance of a contract, often allowing the partial or total exclusion of a contractual clause.*

This Smart really is Smart.

Basically, they are contracts that are programmed to act automatically. They "trigger" automatically upon the occurrence of conditions that are decided a priori.

Acting instantaneously and without having to intervene manually, they can be a great innovation in fields where speed and security are important.

Example? Flight ticket refund: When a plane ticket is bought, a Smart Contract is automatically created aimed at safeguarding your purchase.

As soon as the flight is cancelled, or significantly delayed, the condition written in the Smart Contract is automatically activated.

And like magic: refund! Only those who have suffered a similar misfortune know the refund times of some airlines (if it arrives at all, it's a miracle).

But they can be applied literally to any field, for any purchase based on trust. Trust which today is fundamental for the world economy. It itself is based entirely on trust. Trust in the value and use of a currency or trust in the repayment of a debt.

Without it, a Capitalist market like the modern one could not be sustained. *source: [Sapiens, A Brief History of Humankind – Yuval Noah Harari]*

## Smart Contract: from the cradle to the grave

The life of a Smart Contract can last two minutes or even several years, but it follows the same path in any case.

### 1) Agreement

The contract is stipulated. In this phase, nothing new happens. The clauses are chosen, details decided, and the Smart Contract is written.

### 2) Verification and Block Creation

In this phase, the contract becomes truly Smart: it is recorded on the Blockchain.

And from this moment it becomes executive. The network of devices participating in the network guarantees its maintenance and security.

The Smart Contract is therefore now part of a block, which has been validated by the network's Miners.

Who are the Miners? The network, being a distributed network, works with the cooperation of a large number of people. These people are the Miners.

Miners make their processors (computers) available to support the network.

In fact, it needs computing power to function safely and efficiently. The provided computing power is used precisely to solve complex mathematical operations that allow creating a block of the chain (blockchain – chain of blocks).

Once the block is validated, the information is imprinted forever, immutable, in the Blockchain. It can never be deleted or modified.

### 3) Oracle

The Blockchain is only a shared ledger, it cannot access external events.

Therefore, to verify the occurrence of events triggering the Smart Contract, the Oracle exists.

This mysterious name simply indicates a third element such as an application. It is therefore responsible for notifying the Smart Contract.

### 4) Execution

As soon as the Smart Contract receives the information, the clause triggers. And automatically what was foreseen in writing the contract happens (refund, transfer of ownership, anything).
