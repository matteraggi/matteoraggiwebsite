## What are Futures and how do they work

You must know that **Futures** do not exist only on **Cryptocurrencies** but were born much earlier.

They have always been used in the **investment** world.

**Futures** are **contracts** stipulated between two parties with a **fixed price** and an **expiration** date.

They serve to "**deliver**" an **asset** at a **future** moment agreed upon before at an established price.

**Futures**, however, are **derivative products**, and this means that by investing money in them we will not own the **asset** but will only use its **price**.

So the **underlying asset** is usually a **raw material**, an **index**, or a **financial instrument**.

**Derivatives**, of which **Futures** are indeed part, are very complex instruments, used mainly for speculation by exploiting the [**leverage effect**](https://www.avatrade.it/education/trading-for-beginners/guide-to-leverage).

The leverage effect allows us to **amplify** the movements that the asset price will undergo, both negatively and positively.

It is real **leveraged trading**, which can be used as **hedging**, but above all as **speculation**.

The **Futures** we will deal with are part of the [**Currency Futures**](https://www.borsaitaliana.it/borsa/glossario/currency-future.html) category. The payment of Futures, on one side or the other, will then be made at the **end of the contract**.

In any case, if you are a beginner, I must advise you against operating with **Futures** for speculative purposes, especially by exploiting very high leverage.

Let's go more specifically and try to understand **what Crypto Futures are**.

## What are Crypto Futures

**Crypto Futures** work the same way as the standard type of Futures, only they are on **Cryptocurrencies**. Very often on **Bitcoin**.

Needless to say, their use in this case is purely for **speculation**, for this you must be careful.

It is an instrument that I would recommend to **expert traders** and that I myself prefer not to use.

Like any **instrument** used with **leverage**, they are even more dangerous with **volatile** assets like **Crypto**. Which moreover are not even properly **regulated**.

Ultimately it is a **bet**, a **prediction** of the future price.

## Futures Features

- Everyone can participate in the **Futures** market and this provides **a lot of liquidity** of fundamental importance
- The **leverage** used can be very high and the closing of many **Futures** can move the price of an **Asset** even faster
- It is a **transparent market** as all operations are public
- **Futures** apply and have always applied to numerous **assets** both **digital** and **physical**
- It is a **strategy** that allows earning with very low **initial costs**

## Crypto Futures Trading

After understanding what **Crypto Futures** are, let's move on to practice.

They are instruments that as I have already mentioned are used in **Trading**.

They are used for a strategy, especially on Cryptocurrencies, of **Scalping**. So very **fast** operations, of **very short term**.

If you plan to use them remember that most **traders** lose money in the markets. And many have been studying the subject for years.

Certainly, for those in the trade, they are **useful** and interesting instruments, which can allow the application of various **strategies**.

## Futures: practical example

Today you open a **Bitcoin Future** at the current price of $20,000. A **long** operation of 1 Bitcoin with a duration of 3 days.

After 3 days a **Bitcoin** is worth $25,000. You bought Bitcoins at $20,000 so you "saved" or "**earned**" $5,000.
