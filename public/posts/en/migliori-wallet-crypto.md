## What is a Crypto Wallet in short

Let's start from the beginning. If you searched for "Best Crypto Wallets" you probably already know what I'm talking about, but I still want to give you a brief initial explanation.

Just as cash is kept in a physical wallet, **Cryptocurrencies** are kept in virtual wallets.

They serve both to store currencies and to be able to trade them. The metaphor with the physical wallet still holds.

**Online security**, however, plays a much more complex role than security in the real world.

This is because the online issue becomes decidedly more abstract.

If offline it is "enough" to keep money in a safe place (like banks or safes), online there are several paths to explore.

It is time to talk about the **types of Wallets** that exist. Because yes, there are multiple types.

## There are multiple types of Wallets

A **Crypto Wallet** can be distinguished according to two main categories:

- If it is Hot or Cold
- If it is Custodial or Non-Custodial

These differences totally change the role and characteristics of the Wallet.

Thanks to the different existing types we can use them for very different purposes. Ranging from storing them in a completely safe place to using them to operate on any [DApps](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/web-3-0-dapps/) (Decentralized Applications).

### Hot - Cold

This first difference divides Wallets into:

- **Hot**, Online Wallets
- **Cold**, Offline Wallets

**Online wallets** are all those wallets that allow you to operate on different platforms.

So wallets that allow you to buy Cryptocurrencies on **Exchanges**, that allow you to buy [NFTs](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/nft-reali-utilizzi/) or that allow you to operate on any Web 3 application.

From the description I just gave, they might seem like the best choice, but they also have a downside.

That is, being Online, therefore connected to the network, they are exposed to dangers of **Hacker attacks**.

Which are quite frequent. Just search online for "Crypto Hacker Attack" and you won't struggle to find news about it.

What is the solution?

The second type of wallet we talk about: [Cold Wallets](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/cold-wallet/).

**Offline wallets** are truly Offline, so they are not connected to the network at all.

It is not possible to communicate through them with exchange platforms or DApps.

They serve exclusively as a **safe**.

Every time you connect to the network, like every time you go out, an Online wallet puts all your Cryptocurrencies at risk.

In fact, most of your tokens should stay safe in a **Cold Wallet**.

Also remember that Bitcoin was created to be used as a store of value. It is therefore necessary to have a safe place to keep it.

The two types of **Wallets** are two sides of the same coin, both necessary for the development of this world.

### Custodial - Non-custodial

There is a type of wallet where your **Tokens** really shouldn't be. To understand what type of wallet I'm talking about, we need to make a further distinction.

Hot Wallets are in turn divided into:

- **Custodial**
- **Non-Custodial**

The wallets to avoid absolutely are the first ones.

Custodial Wallets are wallets managed by **third parties**. Centralized entities that guard the Cryptocurrencies we deposit on our behalf.

What does this mean in practice?

You must know that wallets possess **two keys**: a public one and a private one.

The public key serves to identify the wallet on the network. So to **receive Crypto**, for example.

The private key is to be kept private indeed and serves to access our wallet.

This key should be kept personally in a safe place, usually physical. And also resistant to time.

In the case of **Custodial Wallets** this does not happen. The key is kept by third-party entities in our place.

This is certainly convenient for the average user, but it goes against every principle with which **Cryptocurrencies** were created.

Using this system we are not giving more power to the individual user, we are simply moving it from traditional banks to new **Exchanges**.

In the case of Non-Custodial Wallets instead, we must keep our private key personally. It is a big responsibility but it gives us real power over our **money**.

Furthermore, it is extremely safer.

I advise you to keep it **non-digitally** and in the most time-resistant material possible (not on a toilet paper roll, to give you an idea).

If **Hot - Non-Custodial** wallets are very useful when used correctly, Hot - Custodial wallets are to be avoided as much as possible.

Obviously, it is not a problem to hold Cryptocurrencies on platforms of this type if it involves small amounts.

## What are the best Cold Wallets

[Cold Wallets](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/cold-wallet/), as I had already mentioned before, are necessary.

One should not underestimate the danger of holding all one's **tokens** only on a Hot Wallet.

But there are two types of Cold Wallets:

- **Software** Wallet
- **Hard** Wallet

The difference is that one is digital while the other is physical.

Both are Cold Wallets so they are very safe. The **Hard Wallet** however is even better from this point of view.

This is because in a Software Wallet our information is kept on the hard drive of the device we are using.

And the device we use to access it is connected to the **Internet**.

While a Hard Wallet is not connected to the network at all, so it is not possible in any way for a **Hacker** to access it.

That said, both types will be present in the list. To evaluate them I used these three categories:

- Cost
- Security
- Ease of use

It is a very **secure** wallet with an interface suitable for anyone.

It allows even those who have just interfaced with the world of cryptocurrencies to keep them in a safe place, breaking down the barriers to entry to **Cold Wallets**, very little used.

It is a free Software Wallet.

## How to use a Crypto Wallet safely

This is not intended to be a detailed guide on how to use a **Crypto wallet**. It is meant to be more of a list of **tips** on how to use it safely.

1. Do not share the Seed Phrase
2. Write the **Seed Phrase** in a safe place: paper and difficult to damage
3. **Keep** your Hardware wallet in a safe place
4. Keep most of your Cryptocurrencies in an Offline Wallet
