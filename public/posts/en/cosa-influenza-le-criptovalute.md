## Beyond the tip of the Iceberg

What we know for sure is that there are many factors that move the price of **Crypto** on the market. But you must know that they all lead to the same thing.

So we could say that the value of any Crypto is decided by one and only one factor.

Do you want to know what it is?

It is the relationship between **supply and demand**.

You must know that every **Asset** is influenced by this value.

High supply and low demand? Low price.

Low supply and high demand? High price.

Simple enough.

Except that we want to dig deeper. I promised you, didn't I?

What we really need to discover is what moves demand.

What pushes people to **buy** or sell. This is complicated.

Every day **€14,325,357,580** in Bitcoin are exchanged *(July 2023 data)*. So asking every person why they did it doesn't seem possible.

However, we can understand generally what the factors are that move the price of our beloved **Crypto**.

## What Influences Cryptocurrencies (Really)

We have reached the crux of the matter. These main factors could significantly influence our **investments**.

Let's start.

### Correlation with traditional markets

Here is a classic. **Bitcoin**, and consequently the entire Crypto market, is often accused of not having its own trend.

But simply following the American **stock market**.

We are here to find out if it's true.

Many analyses have been done on this subject that can help us discover if it is really so.

A factor that makes it truly complicated to draw a definitive conclusion is the fact that this **correlation** is not stable.

If there is one thing we know for sure, it is that at some times it is greater than others.

So conflicting **news** often comes out:

- "**Crypto market** increasingly correlated with the stock market"
- "Correlation between **Bitcoin** and the stock market vanished"

And they are churned out continuously.
In these cases we must:

1. Not trust all the news we read
2. Do our own research and draw conclusions

And that is exactly what I am about to do.

Why?

There are those who would answer: "because **Bitcoin**, which is not a real investment Asset, simply follows the stock market".

But I don't see it that way.

Bitcoin is still "small". By now many know it, but very few really know what it is.

And this means that most people who buy it do so only for pure short-term **speculation**.

And when are people most stimulated to speculate **upwards**?

When the market is going up obviously. When there is **Bullish** air, when the market rises.

And so Bitcoin rises too, but it is a synchronization of speculations, due to the temporary **polarization** of a major market on a minor one.

The more **Cryptocurrencies** become known and studied, the more this correlation will decrease.

But for the moment yes, like all minor markets based on supply and demand, Crypto is partly **influenced** by the American stock market.

### Big Movements by large Whales

Do you know what **Whales** are? If you know, skip the next 2 lines.

[Whales](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/terminologia-crypto/), in a stock market, are... whales. So those who have an invested **capital** (or to invest) so big as to move an entire market by themselves.

In a small market like the **Crypto** one, these "whales" make themselves heard more.

Relax, we are not talking about power such as to cause a **-10%**. But on Crypto they have a double role.

As I already told you, just by operating with their capital they can slightly move the **price**, but that's not all.

As you may already know, Cryptocurrencies are built on a **Decentralized Database** that is public. In simple terms?

Anyone can see any movement made on the market.

Consequently, if a big whale makes an important movement, those who follow [on-chain](https://www.crypto.it/2022/06/23/analisi-on-chain-metriche-activity/) movements will surely find out. And this **influences** other people too.

PS: Although it is a public database, the privacy factor is also present. Each movement is described through wallet addresses, personal names are not present.

### Inflation in crisis countries

**Inflation**. A slow but lethal weapon.

Silent and dangerous. Inflation is capable of bringing us to ruin, consuming the money resulting from our work.

In the main Western countries, this problem has been mitigated in recent years. And the **damage** it caused is not too serious.

There are countries that are not so lucky though. Whole populations victims of terrible **monetary decisions**.

The solution in these cases is **Bitcoin**.

But I'm not the one saying it, creator of a Website on Cryptocurrencies, the **data** says it.

I present [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com). It is a very interesting tool, it is a **database** of the Blockchain.

Well, according to a 2021 report of theirs, Cryptocurrency usage increased by **880%**.

But where did it increase the most? In **emerging markets**, in those less developed countries.

And also where inflation is out of control.

In 2022 they built this chart based on numerous data to classify countries in order of **Cryptocurrency usage**.

One immediately notices how most of the countries in the high part of the ranking are part of the category I described previously.

So they are:

- Countries in **crisis**
- **Emerging** Markets

This phenomenon, although slow and gradual, will bring great benefits in the use of **Cryptocurrencies** over time.
