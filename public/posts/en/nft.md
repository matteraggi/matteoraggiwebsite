## What are NFTs

The acronym NFT stands for **Non-Fungible Token**, which literally means non-fungible Tokens.

A non-fungible token is unique and is not interchangeable; in practice, **only one** exists.

This is the first difference from cryptocurrencies (with which they are sold and bought), because they are "coins", created to be exchanged and to circulate, while NFTs are **unique**, each one different from the other.

This is the explore page of the largest NFT trading site, [Opensea](https://opensea.io/).

They are mainly used for what made them famous, namely **digital art**: images, videos and also audio, but mainly the first two.

Furthermore, now with the explosion of the [metaverse](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/metaverso) also NFTs are expanding, being closely connected.

If you want to know why they are connected, visit my article about it: "[NFTs and Metaverse: what they are and why they are connected](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/nft-metaverso/)"

Like on the [The Sandbox platform](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/the-sandbox) which is exploding lately with the release of its metaverse, where it is possible to sell and buy non-fungible tokens.

So simply put they are unique photos and videos, which by buying you can exclusively possess.

They are **paintings**... but digital.

## How they get value: the Blockchain

So let's go back to the previous discussion... how does a photo get value?

As many say, with a simple screenshot you can have the same photo in your gallery without paying a euro, so it makes no sense for it to have value.

But the truth is that this statement makes no sense for one main reason: the **Blockchain**.

Everything that this technology is capable of doing is probably still **underestimated** and unexploited.

Very few people are informing themselves about what the **future** could be in almost all fields of work, because people have not yet understood its **potential**.

I cannot talk about the Blockchain in this article, because it is a topic too complicated and long and needs its own reserved space.

I will certainly talk about it in the future and I invite you to **inform yourselves** because when everyone knows what it is about, it will already be **too late**.

However, to make you understand what it is about I can tell you that the ownership of an NFT is **registered** and associated with the wallet of the buyer and it is impossible to "rig" this process.

The Blockchain in fact makes all movements **public**. It is not possible to **counterfeit** a digital work of art.

The Blockchain is the technology that is the basis of [Web 3.0](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/web-3-0/), a revolution of which NFTs are a part.
