There is a lot of talk about how **Bitcoin** could be used as the **main currency**, instead of the dollar and the euro.

Used for everyday transactions: to pay for a coffee or to buy a book.

Especially with the **Lighting Network**, this could be, on paper, also feasible.

But many doubts remain. Also because Bitcoin was not created for this.

**Bitcoin**, as it was structured, is better suited for larger and less frequent transactions.

So it is more of a store of value. It resembles **gold** more than state currencies (FIAT).

Despite this, many Bitcoin supporters want to use it as the sole **currency of exchange**.

But is it really possible? Maybe today it is still a bit complicated.

We will try to understand it in this article. What can I **buy with Bitcoin** (today)?

## It depends on...

It depends on where we are. The case of **El Salvador**, a Central American country that decided to make Bitcoin a legal tender, is now famous.

What does it mean? That every merchant is obliged to accept it.

So if we are in El Salvador, we can buy everything.

But I don't think many readers of this article are there right now.

I will analyze what can be bought with **Bitcoin** in the main countries of the world and mainly in **Italy**.

## Alternatives

There are already services that allow you to "**pay everywhere with Bitcoin**" even if it is not exactly like that.

They allow you to hold the **Cryptocurrency** and then automatically change it into Euro/Dollar/... at the time of payment.

This can be convenient but it is certainly not the same as operating a transaction on **Blockchain**.

## What can I buy with Bitcoin?

I will surprise you, the options are more than you think.

But obviously, businesses that accept **Bitcoin** are still just a small niche. Furthermore, those who are doing it have chosen not to expose themselves too much and not sponsor the thing.

There are many activities therefore that have started doing it "on trial".

### Video Games

Video games are inevitably very linked to **Cryptocurrencies**. Many gaming platforms use fictitious tokens to regulate game exchanges.

The step from game [token](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/token-cosa-sono/) to token with real value is not that big.

I can imagine that in a few years the niche of **play to earn** video games already existing will grow even more.

Some of the businesses that accept Bitcoin are:

- [G2A](https://www.g2a.com/it/): The most important digital store in the world for the sale of video game codes.
- [Instant-gaming](https://www.instant-gaming.com/it/): This digital store, also specialized in the sale of video game codes for multiple platforms, allows payment via cryptocurrencies like bitcoin.
- [Joltfun](https://joltfun.com/): It is another website where you can buy games for different platforms using your Bitcoin. They also offer support for payments via Lightning Network.
- [Games Planet](https://uk.gamesplanet.com/): Another option that accepts Bitcoin as a means of payment and with a wide catalog of games at your fingertips.

### IT

The **IT** / technology sector is certainly very active in this area. In fact, there are also large companies that offer the possibility of payments in **Crypto**.

Clearly, companies are guided by market demand, and IT product customers are more inclined than others to use **Bitcoin**.

- [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/it-it): Allows selecting bitcoin as a payment method. To do this, just access the “payments and billing” section and within “payment options” select “redeem bitcoin”. In this way, you can purchase Microsoft products paying with your wallet.
- [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com): Offers domain name registrations and web hosting. The Namecheap account can be topped up with Bitcoin / Bitcoin Cash / Ethereum / XRP funds, provided the wallet used is compatible with BitPay.
- [Express VPN](https://www.expressvpn.com/it): One of the most known and used VPN services after Nord VPN.
- [Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.it): It is one of the most well-known hosting service websites. It offers the possibility to pay for all services provided using bitcoin.
- [WordPress](https://wordpress.com/it/): The largest blog platform in the world. It also accepts bitcoin payments for the services offered.

### Online Shopping

The goal for an **e-commerce** is always to make the purchase as simple as possible for the customer. Offering every payment method, even Bitcoin, is certainly useful to attract new interested people.

- [Overstock](https://www.overstock.com): It is mainly dedicated to selling products related to home decoration (furniture, bedding, and other products) although it is also possible to find new products. But it is available with delivery only in America.
- [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com): It is a global marketplace of unique and creative items. It is used for selling usually handmade products.
- [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/it): The number 1 platform for building e-commerce sites. The platform supports over 300 digital currencies.
- [Showroomprivato](https://www.saldiprivati.com): If you are a fashion fan but want to save by buying products from big brands, you can do it here and pay in bitcoin.

### Giftcards

I could still put **GiftCards** in the "alternatives" section. In the end, you buy a simple gift card that you can then use on the site in question.

- [BitRefill](https://www.bitrefill.com/it/it/): Allows using Bitcoin to purchase gift cards from various stores. They also offer support for the Lightning Network to make instant payments.
- [eGyfter](https://www.egifter.com/): It is another gift card sales company that accepts payments in Bitcoin.
- [Gyft](https://www.gyft.com/): Company dedicated to selling gift cards.

### Donations

Even non-profit associations that ask for donations have innovated. Many have decided to accept **donations** in Bitcoin.

- [Wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Portada): The largest encyclopedia in the world, supported by volunteer users. Accepts donations also in Bitcoin.
- [Save the Children](https://www.savethechildren.it): The non-profit organization fighting for the most disadvantaged children allows donating cryptocurrencies or using its wallets for people to mine bitcoin for them.
- [BitHope](https://bithope.org/): Nice initiative that allows making donations in bitcoin and following the evolution of such donations at all times.
- [BitGive](https://www.bitgivefoundation.org/): Another charitable organization where it is possible to make donations using bitcoins.

### Real Estate Market

The first purchase in the world of a house with **Bitcoin** took place in September 2017, in Austin, Texas. And in 2018 in Italy, in Turin.
Although Bitcoin is not a currency with **legal tender** in Italy, if there is an agreement between the parties it can be used as a means of payment.

## Other large companies that accept Bitcoin

### Mastercard

Mastercard has been allowing **Cryptocurrency payments** through its platform for quite some time now. And it did it in the best way.

Instead of trying to sneak into a world that doesn't belong to them, they decided to form partnerships with Services in the sector.

It therefore collaborated with [Metamask](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/metamask/) and MoonPay.

They have also stated several times that they support **Cryptocurrencies** and believe in the sector. Defining them as "much more than simple speculation".

In addition to Metamask, it has formed and is forming partnerships with many **Startups** with which I expect innovative services to come out.

Competitor **Visa** is also taking steps in this direction.

### Gucci

Gucci accepts several **Cryptocurrencies**, but only in American stores for the moment.

### Microsoft

Since way back in 2014, Microsoft started accepting **payments in Bitcoin**. The Cryptocurrency can indeed be used for services like Skype and Xbox Live.

### Paypal

Paypal is one of the largest payment companies in the world. In 2021 it integrated the possibility of using **Cryptocurrencies** for payments.

It is also possible to keep them directly on a [wallet](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/migliori-wallet-crypto/) on the platform thanks to the partnership with Metamask, now the undisputed giant among **Hot Crypto Wallets**.

### Twitch

The digital streaming platform allows making donations with various **Crypto**. In addition to donations, you can also pay the monthly subscription to creators.

### Starbucks

In American Starbucks coffee shops, you can buy in **cryptocurrencies** directly from the app, thanks to the partnership with Bakkt.

### Amazon? Tesla?

Amazon and Tesla could both accept Cryptocurrencies in the near future.

From Amazon, we know that it is looking for **Blockchain experts**, probably for this very reason.

While Tesla had already decided to accept Cryptocurrencies, then backtracking for reasons related to **environmental sustainability**. However, it has stated that in the future it will reintegrate the function, but only when Bitcoin [mining](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/minare-dogecoin/) becomes Greener.

## Differences from country to country

Then there are differences between all the countries of the world.

In countries where **Bitcoin** adoption is very high, all commercial services use it.

So even large multinationals like Coca-Cola for some of its distributors or many McDonalds.

## And in Italy?

To discover all the places that **accept Bitcoin** just consult one of these two sites: [Coinmap.org](https://coinmap.org) or [Quibitcoin](https://www.quibitcoin.it).

They monitor activities that allow **purchasing in Cryptocurrencies**.

As for Online stores, just check that they deliver to Italy.

Also through the sites, it is possible to locate **Bitcoin ATMs**, real ATMs to withdraw cryptocurrency and use it later.

## Conclusion

Although there are probably not many people nowadays who would use Bitcoin to pay for everyday expenses, it is interesting to discover that there are quite a few businesses proposing **Crypto** as a purchase method.

It is certainly an indication of growing trust in the sector, which we hope will increase even in the coming years.
