**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** increases its potential every year, and now we are able to create intelligences that we couldn't even imagine a few years ago.

It is a complicated theme that **intrigues** some and **scares** others.

Its possibilities are many and can be applied to almost any area of our life.

It is a theme that intrigues many because through Artificial Intelligence we can create incredibly **Smart** and intelligent devices, therefore able to do things for us that we were obliged to perform.

But it is also a theme that scares because many think that it will be possible, in a few decades, to reach a point where the **Intelligences** we created surpass us and take control of the world.

Idea perhaps a bit **utopian** but not too much.

So what are the real applications of **Artificial Intelligence in everyday life?**

Let's try to understand it in the course of this article, where we will analyze what are the main applications today.

## Examples of artificial intelligence today

Where is Artificial Intelligence used in everyday life?

Voice Assistants, Search and recommendations, Autonomous Driving, Navigators, App Notifications, Medicine, Translators, Military, Advertisements, Email, ChatBots, and Trading Bots.

### Voice Assistants

[Voice Assistants](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/linternet-delle-cose/) are perhaps the most striking example of this technology. They are essentially an **Artificial Intelligence** and a **speaker** to be able to communicate with human beings.

They are programmed precisely to have a **behavior** as **human** as possible, therefore to understand what we say, comprehend it and formulate an adequate response.

They are also an artificial intelligence very **present in our daily life**.

Here Artificial Intelligence completely does its job, it is as if they were an **artificial brain**.

They are most likely the ancestors of the **robots** we have seen represented in movies for years.

The brain already exists, the implementation of a **body** and its interaction with the brain is missing.

After this last step, what are now our **Voice Assistants**, will become Robots capable of performing many of our tasks like grocery shopping or cleaning the house.

The best-known voice assistants are: [Google](https://store.google.com/it/product/google_nest_mini?hl=it), [Alexa](https://amzn.to/3La5Tsk), and [Siri](https://www.apple.com/homepod-mini/).

### Search and recommendations

Artificial Intelligence is also used in **Social Media** and [search engines](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/motore-di-ricerca-cos-e/). Socials such as Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. Search engines like Youtube and Google.

In **Social Networks**, it is used with **recommended posts**, the intelligence through a series of parameters proposes content that you might like based on those you have already appreciated on the platform.

It is therefore able to understand what are the **similar contents** through a sequence of data and information it analyzes.

In Search Engines, instead, it is able to **understand what you are looking for** based on what you searched for before.

If you search for example "root" and previously you consulted mathematics sites, the engine will recommend results regarding the mathematical root and not the root of a plant.

### Autonomous Driving

**Autonomous driving** already exists, but, unlike the previous uses of **AI**, this has not expanded much because it is still immature, but in the future, it will certainly be one of the main **artificial intelligences** in our daily life.

**Artificial Intelligence** is used to understand the structure of the road and its state.

It can understand where the road lanes are, so as to stay in the lane by itself, obviously it knows speed limits and interacts with sensors to understand what is around it.

Furthermore, if the road is not in excellent condition it can understand and signal it, so as to **adapt** **always**.

The most famous car Brand from this point of view is **Tesla**, although there are also other models of other brands that have autonomous driving.

### Navigators

**Navigators** are able to evaluate which is the **fastest route** based on what is happening at that moment. They know how to evaluate the extent of a slowdown or any problems on the road.

With Artificial Intelligence, they choose which is the **best option** and communicate it to you.

Every navigation application on the phone is equipped with this technology: **Google Maps**, **Moovit**, or **Waze**.

### App Notifications

**Applications** study your behaviors when you use the phone and always know the right time to send a notification, such as when you are about to close the phone.

This is because artificial intelligence manages to **understand what your habits are**, so what you usually do before closing the phone or when you use it the most.

**Artificial intelligence algorithms** study all these factors to make you click on their notification, and even if we don't notice it, they cover our entire daily life.

### Medicine

**AI** has been in the medical world for a few years already, particularly applied to **test analysis**.

They can **photographically analyze** many test results, finding diseases and problems. They are also much **faster** than a human being, even if one cannot yet fully rely on them.

They manage to identify genetic diseases and detect tumors in some cases.

### Translators

Artificial intelligence also serves to better understand the meaning of a sentence to **translate it accurately**.

All **translators** exploit it, so they don't translate word for word but the sentence as a whole.

It is also used in the generation of **automatic subtitles**, which usually are not very precise but without **AI** would be even worse.

### Military

Besides being used in all these fields, it is certainly also used **militarily**.

Especially in America where both technological evolution and military spending are very high, there will already be many weapons that exploit this technology to be more **efficient and dangerous**.

The use of artificial intelligences in war can allow attacks without the risk of losing men, acting only with robots.

It is the worst use of this technology for humanity but obviously, like any great creation, it is also used for this.

### Advertisements

Similarly to how search engines and application notifications work, advertisements **study user data** and through them understand their interests.

**Advertisements** will then be shown only to potentially interested users and not to everyone, saving on **advertising spend** and making it much more efficient.

Ads are extremely personalized and precise, increasing their **conversion rate** and all this is possible thanks to AI.

### Email

Emails exploit **artificial intelligence** to sort incoming messages into different categories.

The intelligence filters and divides them into: Spam, Promotions, Social, and Primary. They also manage to understand if there is an event to add to the calendar, and do it automatically.

Most people send emails every day for work, without knowing that it is artificial intelligence that **greatly simplifies their daily life**.

### ChatBot

Don't know what **ChatBots** are?

Impossible, unless you just came out of a Bunker!

Does **ChatGPT** ring a bell?

By now everyone talks about it and everyone is aware of it. It is perhaps the most evident application of **artificial intelligence** currently public.

A Bot that is able to talk to us. But not like Siri who understands nothing...
It really manages to answer sensibly.

It is not the purpose of this article to explain how it does it. But I want to do it briefly anyway.

ChatGPT is a "trained" Artificial Intelligence. This means that it has been subjected to a series of **prompts** (sentences basically) to make it understand how to communicate.

A flood of **data**, but really really a lot. So much that it learned too.

The potential of this type of technology as you can imagine is enormous.

I believe they can greatly simplify human working life in many sectors, increasing **productivity**.

Many are afraid that it might also replace some and it could really be so. But it is not the time yet.

## Artificial Intelligence in the future

After exploring Artificial Intelligence in everyday life we can imagine what it could be like in the **future**.

In the future, we actually don't know what we could do with the use of **Artificial Intelligence**, so you are free to use your imagination.

Many of the things that now seem impossible, in 10 years could be **reality**.

The field of **computer science** evolves with **absurd speed**, much higher than that of other fields.

Suffice it to say that a Ferrari (275 S) had a top speed of 240 km/h in 1950, and in 2010 the fastest Ferrari reached 335 km/h (599XX).

While the first computer (Univac I) which in 1951 had a calculation speed of 1905 op/s, led to the creation in 2010 of the iPad with 2 billion op/s.

If the car world evolved as rapidly as the computer one, a Ferrari would be capable of doing **246 million km/h**.

This is just to show how fast **technological/computer evolution** is.
