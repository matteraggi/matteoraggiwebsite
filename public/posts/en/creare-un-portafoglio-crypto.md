Most **investors** lose money in the market. When the market goes up, everyone **buys**. And when the market goes down, everyone **sells**.

This, as you know, is not the winning method. If you are interested in **how to create a Crypto portfolio** you must think with a long-term perspective.

You need to build a **portfolio** that can perform well over the years.

If most people operate the wrong way, it is because they fall into the trap of **easy money**.

They are tempted to enter the market when they see that a **stock** or a **cryptocurrency** has performed well. This is because they think of all the people who earned "simply" by **investing**.

Do not make this mistake.

It's like buying **lottery** tickets because someone won the week before.

But that's not the trick.

Strokes of luck exist, but that's not how you become **rich**.

To multiply your **investments** in the **long term** you will have to really understand what you are putting your money into. Only in this way will you not be scared by market **fluctuations**.

You must create a balanced **portfolio**, of which you will have to know every **asset** perfectly.

## Market Psychology

If you have been in the world of **Cryptocurrencies** for a few years you will know how public opinions change passing from **Bull** to [**Bear Market**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/terminologia-crypto/).

During the Bull Market everyone wants to know more, everyone is interested in discovering what these **Cryptos** are.

During the Bear Market Crypto becomes the usual market **bubble**, with no value.

I do not want to diminish the opinion of those who do not appreciate **Cryptocurrencies**, but I want to dwell on another thing.

The opinion of a specific **Asset** cannot and must not change with respect to the **market trend**.

The market goes up and the market goes down, this is normal, but the underlying **value** remains the same. It does not vary according to market **fluctuations**.

**Bitcoin** remains Bitcoin and **Ethereum** remains Ethereum.
This applies to both **Crypto** and Stocks.

A good **investor** knows this and is not influenced by it, on the contrary, they exploit it to their advantage.

The fact that I am interested in buying **Meta** stocks, considering the company very solid and cutting-edge, does not change if the price of its **stocks** drops.

If this happened, I would still be interested in buying. And I would be even happier, given the lower **purchase price**.

## How to create a Crypto portfolio: the Assets

To build a **long-term portfolio** you will have to focus on **Assets** that can last over time.

The **Cryptocurrency** market is still expanding strongly and this means that most of them are still poorly established.

For this reason, the composition of a balanced **portfolio** should be:

- **75% Bitcoin**
- **20% Ethereum**
- **5% Altcoin**

With slightly variable percentages.

**Bitcoin** will have the majority of the portfolio for the following reasons:

- it is the most stable **cryptocurrency**
- it **leads** the entire market (altcoins usually follow its trend)
- it is the most **decentralized** asset

The percentage reserved for **Altcoins** must be managed carefully.

I advise you to eliminate it if you are unable to evaluate a **Crypto** project.

Otherwise, if you feel capable of doing so, I advise you to follow these guidelines:

- **Top 20 Crypto** by capitalization

"Top 20" is an indicative figure to make you understand that you should not choose a project that entered the **market** two days ago and is still unknown. But you will have to choose a **reliable** project, despite being an Altcoin.

So no **ShitCoin** and no betting will enter your long-term portfolio.

- Know the project well, **do your research**

It is fundamental to know perfectly every project that is inserted into the **portfolio**. Remember that it will remain with you possibly for many more years.

Obviously, the portfolio can be **modified** at any time, but one of the points in favor of this investment method is precisely that of having a portfolio **unchanged** over the years.

- **Few Altcoins**

You won't have to divide the remaining 5% of the portfolio into 200 **Cryptocurrencies**. There is no need to try at all costs to include any interesting project.

Try to make a **selection** as narrow as possible. Really, the fewer, the better.

The important thing is to be completely sure of the chosen asset.

- **Rebalance** the portfolio

It can happen, more often than one might think, that the portfolio becomes **unbalanced**. A **token** can rise or fall much faster than others, and this damages our **composition**.

Every month make sure to check the **balance** and eventually bring it back to the established percentages.

Attention, it makes no sense to balance the **portfolio** too frequently. You would end up selling too soon assets that still have **growth margin**.

The best choice is to decide beforehand how often to do it. I advise you to wait at least a month, but you can also do it **quarterly**.

## How to create a Crypto portfolio: Diversification

When it comes to **creating a Crypto portfolio** one of the most important factors is its **diversification**.

We can classify **Cryptocurrencies** into several types.

Greater diversification can be obtained by inserting more **types**. Avoiding including in the **portfolio** a large number of **Altcoins**.

### Payment Coins

These are currencies that deal only with **payments**.

They are mainly the **Cryptos** born years ago such as: **Bitcoin**, Bitcoin Cash, Ripple or Litecoin.

After the birth of **Ethereum** and the creation of [**DApps**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/web-3-0-dapps/) few have been created.

### Security Tokens

A **Security Token** can represent any fractional ownership of an **Asset**.

A fraction of a company, therefore **stocks**. Works of art, real estate or objects to **collect**.

Any entity can choose to create and distribute **security tokens**.

They have the same role as a simple **fractionation** into shares or certificates of ownership.

However, it represents a more comfortable and secure option as it removes numerous **obstacles** and slowdowns.

Despite this, I do not recommend exaggerating with Security Tokens at this time.

Indeed, it would be better to avoid them altogether.

In fact, at the moment there are numerous problems between American legislation and Security Tokens. For this reason, many of them could risk **delisting**.

### Utility tokens

**Utility tokens** are probably the tokens that are spreading the most.

They are **tokens** that serve directly to use a **service** or a product.

Tokens of this type are: **Ethereum** or any token of [**Exchange**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/migliori-exchange-crypto/) platforms like **CRO** of Crypto.com, **BNB** of Binance or **YNG** of Young Platform.

Their **value** is obviously linked to the success of the service. If the project does not work, the **token** will lose value.

Utility tokens can be too **centralized**, as in the previous example of **Exchanges**. In that case, you will have to be careful and not hold many.

### Governance tokens

**Governance tokens** are used mainly in the world of **decentralized finance**.

They serve to take part in a **project**. Thanks to a Governance token you can vote and therefore take part in the **decisions** of a **decentralized** project.

Examples of Governance tokens are: **UNI** (token of the decentralized platform Uniswap), **CAKE** (token of Pancakeswap) and [**MANA**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/decentraland/) (token of the virtual world Decentraland).

### AI Tokens

**Cryptocurrencies** and **Artificial Intelligence** are not two such distant worlds, also because **AI** is now contaminating every area of our life.

There are numerous **Crypto** projects that are investing in the development of **Artificial Intelligence**, such as: **TRX** (Tron), **FET** (Fetch.ai) and **GRT** (The Graph).

### GameFi Tokens

The **Gaming** sector is a powerful sector and many underestimate it.

It is also a field very close to **Crypto**, we have already seen it with **play to earn** games.

In particular, **GameFi** games are, as the name says, **Decentralized** (DeFi) Games. And they are a subcategory of play to earn games.

Platforms already known like [**The SandBox**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/the-sandbox/) or **Axie Infinity** are part of it.

The common dynamic is the release of a **token** used as game currency.

But they are not like V-Bucks on **Fortnite** or Fifa Points because these tokens are real **Cryptocurrencies** that we can convert back into **FIAT** currency.

A real internal market is thus created in the video game that can allow us to **earn** real money.

### NFT Tokens

These are a truly narrow niche and **tokens** that have been created based on an [**NFT collection**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/come-creare-collezione-nft/) belong to it. Like for example the **ApeCoin** token.

They are closely linked to the trend of the **collection**.

### Ethereum Ecosystem

Make sure not to include in your portfolio only tokens belonging to the **Ethereum Blockchain**.

This is a mistake that many make as the Ethereum Blockchain is literally conquering the entire **market**.

However, it is important, despite Ethereum being the most established project after **Bitcoin**, not to overexpose oneself to its network.

### Polkadot Ecosystem

Another ecosystem that is gaining consensus is the one based on the **Polkadot network**.

Its system is unique on the market and is formed by specialized Blockchains called **Parachains**.

Some **Cryptos** of the Polkadot ecosystem are: **LINK** (Chainlink), **KSM** (Kusama) and **GLMR** (MoonBeam).

### Storage Tokens

They are **Tokens** that deal with **decentralized storage**. Instead of using cloud storage services from large companies like **Apple** and **Amazon**, these cryptos offer a decentralized solution.

The pros are that it is more protected from **hacker** attacks, that it is more **transparent** and costs less.

Furthermore, the decentralization of the network makes the network **faster**, more efficient and reduces costs.

The main Cryptocurrencies of this type are **Filecoin** and **BitTorrent**.

## How, when and how much to invest in the portfolio

This is a personal choice that depends mainly on economic availability. I would possibly invest on a **monthly** basis.

Use a platform that allows you to **invest** **automatically**, without the need to do it manually.

Set an **amount**. Please make a wise choice, the goal is not to start with a **large investment**, but is to be able to afford that specific amount with certainty for a long time.

When using **periodic** investments, you take advantage of the **average purchase price** which generally manages to remain low.

So focus on the **durability** of your investment, not on the value of the single amount.

The **frequency** can be monthly as well as daily or weekly. The difference is that the greater the frequency the greater the mediation of the **average price**. But **commissions** will also be higher.

Test yourself to see the results that different methods would have obtained in the past.

## Where to hold Crypto

Now that you have decided on the composition of your **portfolio**, you will have to decide where to hold all the **Cryptos** you are going to buy.

The options as always are two: leave them **on the Exchange** or move them to a [**cold wallet**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/cold-wallet/).

If you have read my other articles you will already know what the pros and cons of each method are, but let's review them briefly.

**Exchange**:

- Ease of use
- Speed of exchange
- Convenience

**Wallet**:

- Security
- Real possession of Tokens

If you use a strategy with monthly frequency it is definitely better to move everything to a **Wallet**.

If you use a weekly strategy you can decide to move the **Crypto** only once a month, not to waste too much time.

## How to monitor the portfolio

You can build your **Crypto portfolio** on any **Exchange** or keep your Cryptos on a **Cold Wallet**, but usually advanced monitoring tools are missing.

In no Exchange is there a good system for **monitoring** one's portfolio.

What you need is:

- Control of the overall **portfolio performance**
- Control of the portfolio composition, therefore the **performance of each individual Asset**

In this way, you will know how to **rebalance** the portfolio.

The best tools that can help us do this are [**CoinMarketCap**](https://coinmarketcap.com/) and **CoinGecko**.

They are both **Crypto** market monitoring tools, which also offer what we need.

### CoinMarketCap

In the **Portfolio** section, you can enter your investments. You will always have monitored the composition and trend of each of your **Assets**.

### CoinGecko

Tool very similar to the previous one, it makes no difference using one or the other.

These tools will make price monitoring very simple and you will not have to do anything but check from time to time that the portfolio does not become too unbalanced. In addition to adding the **transactions** you will make.

## Some Extra advice

Most supporters of **Cryptocurrencies** make the big mistake of investing only in them.

The **technological** side and the **financial** side are two different things.

From a technological point of view **Crypto** and **Blockchain** could be fundamental for the shift from the physical world to the **digital** world we are living in these years.

From my point of view there is no doubt that the **Blockchain** is an invention of great impact and necessary for human technological development.

But when it comes to **money** and **Personal Finance** it is another story.

Do not make the big mistake of **investing** all your savings only on this horse. You must build an **investment** portfolio with the same philosophy as Ray Dalio's **All Seasons**.

Build a system that requires the least possible maintenance time and that can have constant positive returns.

Simple it is not, but certainly having only a **crypto** portfolio without even considering **Stocks** and **bonds** is not the correct choice.

What I see more and more often is the big difference between those who do not invest and those who have just started investing.

Those who do not invest, nor have ever informed themselves about it, have a very low **risk tolerance**. They are afraid of losing money and also for this reason keep all their **wealth** liquid.

Those who are learning to invest instead, tend to behave in the opposite way. They have a very high risk tolerance and are not interested in having a **balanced portfolio**.

Under 30 years old it is correct to have a slightly more **risky** investment portfolio, but it must still be **diversified**.

My advice is to think with a **long-term** perspective. We do not know for sure what could happen in the future and we must build a **portfolio** ready for anything.
