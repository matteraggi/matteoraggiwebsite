## Why should I prefer it to classical finance?

As a lover of lists, here is another one:

- Freedom
- Lower costs
- Fewer errors
- Impossibility of "tampering"
- Speed

Let's quickly see point by point.

**Freedom**: Every individual really owns their own money and can do what they want with it. They don't have to rely on a **bank** to protect them, exposing themselves to the risk of crises and **failures**.

Lower **Costs**: Without intermediaries, as is simple to understand, there are also fewer costs. So for the user, it is convenient to use this **solution**.

Fewer **errors**: The equation is this: **humans = errors**, or to err is human, as they often say. So, without human beings, one exposes oneself to many fewer risks and problems. A computer, once bugs are resolved, is almost impossible to make mistakes.

Impossibility of "**tampering**": For the same previous reason, if there are no human beings, there can be no cheating.

**Speed**: Since everything is done automatically, times are much faster. Not only in **transactions** and normal operations but in anything. You won't even have to wait for Monday for bank transfers to arrive.

## Decentralized Exchanges...

Here we are at the core of the issue: **Decentralized Exchanges**.

Just a small piece of the Decentralized Finance puzzle.

(but a great piece of the puzzle)

These **platforms** play a role similar to:

- banks
- centralized exchanges

In what way are they different?

Banks serve to store, exchange, and trade **FIAT** currencies.

FIAT currencies are the **state currencies** we all know and use (Euro, Dollar, Yen,...).

Then there are [Centralized Exchanges](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/migliori-exchange-crypto/). They exchange Cryptocurrencies, therefore **decentralized** currencies.

Currencies devoid of control by the state. Like **Bitcoin**, Ethereum, Polkadot, and many others.

However, these platforms are still Centralized.

So, even if they trade Cryptocurrencies, they are controlled by people. People who created them and own **shares** of those companies.

That is why true supporters of Cryptocurrencies do not use these platforms except to buy **Crypto** and then move them immediately.

They are platforms subject to problems similar to those of banks.

And then there are **decentralized platforms**.

These platforms, called **DEXs**, are only created but not managed. They are birthed and then abandoned (that sounds a bit bad).

But the concept is this. Once created, they **self-manage**.

They are therefore a category apart, their functioning is completely different from the previous examples.

They give the possibility to exchange Cryptocurrencies in a **peer-to-peer** way without intermediaries.

Peer-to-Peer means user to user. Like a **handshake**, buyer and seller in direct contact.

And they are fundamental.
