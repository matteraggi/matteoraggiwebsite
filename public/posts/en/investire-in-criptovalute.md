## What are Crypto?

Crypto-Currencies. The hyphen serves to separate two parts of the same word.

"Crypto" stands for **Cryptography**. An example is this:

`VAIRFGVERVAPEVCGBINYHGR`

It is incomprehensible. Because it is a phrase I encrypted. Only those who know how to decrypt it will be able to understand its meaning.

If I didn't give you the answer, you would never be able to understand it.

Alright, I'll show you the solution. I used a table that swaps each letter with another.

With this table, anyone can decrypt the message above with a little effort. Anyway, I'll save you the work:

encrypted phrase: `VAIRFGVERVAPEVCGBINYHGR`

clear text: `INVESTIREINCRIPTOVALUTE` (INVEST IN CRYPTOCURRENCIES)

This is a trivial example of what Cryptography means. The mechanisms behind Cryptocurrencies are actually much more complex.

"Currencies" simply indicates that they are means to **exchange value**, like FIAT currencies (Euro, Dollar, Yen,...).

Combining the two pieces of the Puzzle you can better understand their definition:

"**Cryptocurrencies** are digital currencies based on Cryptography. They are currencies that do not exist in physical form and are not managed by central authorities (banks) but by the **Blockchain**"

The question arises spontaneously: What is the Blockchain?

### The Blockchain in short

**Chain of blocks** is the translation. These blocks contain the network data and all transactions are saved there.

They then connect to each other forming a chain.

This particular structure serves to provide a high level of **security**. The block, once created, can no longer be modified.

We therefore have the guarantee that Crypto is a secure means of exchange.

But what should surprise you the most is that this technology can replace the entire banking system. It works without a central control entity.

## The 4 fundamental points

Pay attention, because these 4 points will be fundamental for every **investment plan** of your life. Whether we talk about Crypto or other Assets.

The 4 points are:

- Time Horizon
- Financial Goal
- Risk Tolerance
- Investment Mode

They are the foundations of your "career" as an investor. They will be your main instruments for building every **Investment strategy**.

Remember that an Investment without a strategy is not an Investment. It is playing the lottery. You must always keep these 4 pillars in mind.

Take a sheet of paper and divide it into 4 columns, so as to put on paper what you intend to do.

At the end of this article, reading the paper, you will be ready to **invest**.

Are you ready? Let's start.

### Time Horizon

The investment strategy starts from this decision. But don't worry, it's not a complex decision.

You have to think about how long you intend to keep positions open on a specific **Asset**.

You can decide time horizons like: a few months, 1 year, or even several tens of years.

But the metric that commands is another. It will be the **Financial Goal** that decides more when to close open positions.

Precisely for this reason, the time horizon is more of a range.

It serves to give an idea. To understand what our intentions are.

A strategy to build our future wealth can have a time horizon of even 30/40/50 years. In these cases, it can be simplified and simply said that it is a **long-term investment**.

### Financial Goal

"I sell when it goes up". How many times have I heard this phrase.

It is the classic statement of those who lose money in the markets. If you said it too, I forgive you. But only because you are reading this guide.

Not having a **price target** is the first step to getting lost and not knowing what to do anymore. When the price goes up, we want more.

But unfortunately, the market does not always grow. The curve is more like a series of ups and downs.

A price limit is important even on the downside. It can save you from big losses.

This also depends on your **time horizon**. If it is long-term you shouldn't care about small daily movements, regardless of the price.

Are you starting to notice? The pillars are not standalone. Instead, they operate in a coordinated manner.

### Risk Tolerance

Could you withstand large crashes? Easy to say yes now.

Imagine you just invested **€1,000 in Bitcoin**. You believe it is a good time to enter the market, and you are happy to have done so.

A few days later your €1,000 is no longer 1000.
It is 140.

It is important to understand what our risk tolerance is, without overestimating the ability to **control emotions**.

Risk tolerance serves to decide:

- Investment Mode
- Asset Allocation

Your plans will never materialize as you think, you must always be ready for the unexpected. You must consider the possibility of suffering a crash like the one described.

It is a concept expressed in the book "[The Black Swan: The Impact of the Highly Improbable](https://amzn.to/3KtZ6rO)" by Nassim Taleb.

Reading it can help you build a **long-term investment plan**.

Before the next paragraph remember: most people think they have a **risk tolerance** higher than the real one. It is always better to be prudent.

### Investment Mode

Of the 4 pillars, it is certainly the most practical. There are different **investment modes**:

- Lump Sum Purchase (PIC)
- Recurring Purchase (PAC/DCA)
- Mixed Strategy
- Market Timing / Trading

You must discover which is the most suitable for you.
Stay connected, now I will help you discover it.

#### Lump Sum Purchase (PIC)

It is the most common type. A **single purchase** at a random moment in the market.

Assume you bought exactly at a peak.

As you may have already understood, it is a risky technique.

"But if I invest long-term I shouldn't care what happens immediately after"

Yes, that is correct. But it remains a risky and often inconvenient technique. Especially compared to the strategy we are about to see.

PS: It is a technique that I advise against only for **main Assets** (Bitcoin in a Crypto portfolio).
For secondary investments, it remains valid.

#### Recurring Purchase (PAC/DCA)

Every month (or week) a fixed amount is **invested** automatically. We distribute the investment over the months.

This strategy has numerous benefits:

- price averaging
- gradual increase of invested capital
- less stress and fewer thoughts

But it also has cons.

If the price goes up immediately after the start of the plan then it becomes inconvenient compared to the single purchase.
And you will have to pay attention to **commissions**.

“The stock market is a device to transfer money from the impatient to the patient.”

A very famous phrase by Warren Buffet, the greatest investor in history.

Why do I quote this?

An **Accumulation Plan** (DCA) might seem boring, and it is, but it is probably one of the best tools for long-term investing.

#### Mixed Strategy

Initial investment + Accumulation Plan

It can be done. Just pay attention to the **initial amount**: if it is too high, the subsequent accumulation plan loses meaning.

#### Market Timing

There are those who say it works. And those who say it doesn't.

**Market Timing** consists of understanding the best time to invest through technical analysis.

So rather than investment, we start talking about **Trading**, which is a real profession.

It requires learning [technical analysis](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/guida-trading-base/): study of figures and indicators.

But not being a passive management of assets, it is not a true method for investing.

## Deposit in a Cold Wallet

Remember the story from before?

Now we deal with the resolution of the second point: "Do not hold Cryptocurrencies on Exchanges".

We just have to decide where to hold them. To understand this, let's distinguish between two types of wallet:

- Hot Wallet
- Cold Wallet

**Hot Wallets** are online wallets, like those of Exchanges. They have the following characteristics:

- Direct connection with the network
- Fast Sale and Purchase
- Risk of hacker attack
- Less responsibility (private keys kept by the platform)

**Cold Wallets** instead are Offline wallets, disconnected from the network. Their characteristics are:

- No connection with the network
- Impossibility to exchange crypto without going through Hot Wallet
- No risk of hacker attack
- Greater responsibility of the individual user (possession of keys)

You will have already understood that the first type is more suitable for Trading and small sums, while the second for investments and larger sums.

Then there are two different types of Cold Wallets: software and physical wallets.

Physical wallets, called **Hard Wallets**, are even safer. They are not installed on any device, so they are inaccessible.

The best Cold Wallets are:

- Guarda Wallet
- Atomic Wallet
- Keepkey
- Ledger (hardware)
- Trezor (hardware)

Creating one of these wallets is a simple operation that won't take much time.

Just remember to write down your **private key** (possibly on paper) and keep it in a safe place.

The method to move Crypto from one **wallet** to another is universal, being related to the Blockchain and not the platform. I'll show you now.

It is a simple Crypto transaction.

To make a transfer you will need to know:

- The crypto you want to move
- The network on which to make the transaction
- Public key of the destination wallet

The transaction network is always relative to the Crypto I want to move.

Ethereum you move on Ethereum network, Bitcoin on Bitcoin network. For secondary Cryptocurrencies, just search on the Internet which network they belong to, but the platform shouldn't allow you to select a wrong one.

Finally, just enter the **alphanumeric** code (public key) of the wallet and send.
Be careful not to mistake the code, there is no going back.
