In this article, we will delve into the operation of the most used platform of [Web 3.0](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/web3/) for buying and selling NFTs: **Opensea**.

**How does Opensea work?**

Don't worry, even if it is a new **platform**, it isn't very complicated.

The functions are not many yet and you just need to understand one or two slightly more difficult steps to be able to **navigate** and buy quietly.

## Opensea: Interface and Features

The Home page immediately presents **three possibilities**: explore, create, and learn more about Opensea.

If you scroll down, it shows you the **drops** of famous collections, the **ranking** of the collections that have sold the most, the **trends**, and a small **tutorial**.

The **main menu** consists of:

- Explore
- Stats
- Resources
- Create
- Profile
- Wallet

### Explore

The **explore** section proposes what are ultimately the most purchased collections divided into categories.

If you click on "**All NFTs**" instead of the division into sections, you will have single **NFTs** recommended by the site, as if it were the **explore section** of Instagram, and you can find all kinds.

### Stats

In this section, you can see the complete ranking of the **best sellers** for the period of time you desire: from the last 24 hours to the all-time history.

You can also see the latest activities, so the latest **NFTs** listed on the platform in real-time.

### Resources

In this part of the site, you will find: the **help center**, information on the status of the site, the community on Discord, their Blog, and their Newsletter. In addition to their social pages.

### Create

The most important function if your goal is to sell is precisely this one. By connecting a **wallet**, you can add any NFT you want, simply by adding it and inserting some information.

Later in the article, I will explain in detail the process to create a collection and add **single NFTs**.

### Profile

From the profile, you can see all the **NFTs** you have uploaded to the platform and those you have liked, and you can also access the settings to change some settings or **log out**.

## Opensea: How to sell an NFT

First of all, it is necessary to have a [crypto wallet](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/dove-conservare-criptovalute/) on one of the platforms listed on Opensea (the most famous and reliable are **Metamask** and **Trust Wallet**) and connect it to the platform: the process shouldn't be difficult, with the chrome extension of [Metamask](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/metamask/) it is almost instantaneous.

If you want to create a single Item just go to "**Create**" and from there insert the necessary information to add it, while if you want to create a collection of **NFTs** (even if you don't upload them all together) you have to go to the "**My collection**" section of the user profile.

Then you will have to add each element of the **NFT Collection** individually.

When you add an item to the platform, you will also have to choose the Blockchain to use between **Ethereum** and **Polygon**.

Even if **Ethereum** is more known, be careful selecting it because there are differences.

In fact, Ethereum requires the payment of a **Gas Fee** (transaction tax) for the first item published by a specific wallet.

It is also substantial; in my case, I would have had to pay **$239.89**, while Polygon is **free**.

However, on the Ethereum Blockchain, it is possible to sell NFTs in the form of **auctions**, thus ensuring it to the highest bidder, while on Polygon it is not possible.

After adding the element, you need to put it on sale, simply by selecting the price and the time of the offer which can last **maximum 6 months**.

## Opensea: How to buy an NFT

It is not complicated to buy an NFT; you can buy it immediately or in some cases make an **offer**.

You can also choose whether to pay with **credit card** or with **crypto**. Just click on the item you want to purchase and complete all the **verifications**.

You will be able to buy Crypto of all kinds, such as videos, gifs, audio, photos, images of any kind, and also objects usable in a [Metaverse](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/metaverso).

Read my article on [NFTs and Metaverse](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/nft-metaverso/).

Among the most profitable sellers are indeed two **Metaverse** platforms like [The sandbox](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/the-sandbox) and Decentraland, which sell many NFTs but often inexpensive ones like **skins** for the Avatar or a **decorative object**.

[Ledger](https://shop.ledger.com/?r=b3cfdb42defc) is a hardware wallet that has integrated the possibility of storing NFTs as well.

As with Cryptocurrencies, a hardware wallet allows you to hold your NFTs in a safe place.

## FAQ

**How much do you pay on Opensea?**

Opensea takes a 2.5% commission on every sale. In addition to that, depending on the Cryptocurrency you use, there will be commissions to pay. Now, with the Ethereum 2.0 update, they are relatively low.

**How much does it cost to create an NFT on OpenSea?**

Creating an NFT is a completely free process. From production to distribution without spending a single Euro.

**How much is an NFT worth in euros?**

To find out how much an NFT is worth in Euros, you have to search on the internet for "ETH-EUR" and based on the exchange rate at the moment you will be able to see how much you are going to pay.
But Opensea allows you to see the equivalent value in dollars directly from their site, and 1$ = 1€ right now.
