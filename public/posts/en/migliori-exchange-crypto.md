#### Trading Functions

Let's start with the first: Trading.

I warn you that it is complicated even just to try all the functions that Binance offers.
The main ones are:

* Spot
* Margin / Leverage
* Futures
* Grid Trading
* Demo Account

**Spot Trading** is classic market trading. Without leverage and with operations such as: limit, market, and OCO (One Cancel the Other).

Margin Trading instead exploits **leverage**, which on Binance reaches up to 5x.

The section is managed with a separate account. You can move money from one part of the platform to another very quickly with the "Transfer" function.

In the **Futures** section, you can operate using this type of contract. These trades also use leverage.

Futures are contracts that establish a certain exchange price at a future date.

They are often used as an **investment protection tool** in times of uncertain market.

You can in fact decide to protect your **Bitcoin investments** for a certain period of time with a short.

It is also a market with a lot of liquidity.

**Grid Trading**, on the other hand, is much less known.

It can be done on both the Futures market and the Spot market.

It involves automatic trading, thanks to Bots that you can set up.

It can be an interesting option to explore, but you will have to learn the instructions to give.

Finally, the **demo account**, which you probably already know. It is a virtual account with which you can practice trading.

It is all fake money that the platform makes available to you.

#### Investment Functions

Second part: functions for Investing

These are even more than the previous ones for **Trading**.

Here are the main ones:

* Buy Crypto
* Earn
* Binance Pool
* Launchpool
* Card
* Airdrop

The "**Buy Crypto**" function is nothing new.

It is the simple function where you can buy the Cryptos you want through market operations.

The "**Earn**" section, now present in every Exchange, is where methods to earn passively with your Cryptos are enclosed.

By now it is a section present in every platform. However, it differs in passive earning methods.

On **Binance**, in fact, there are various types of "Earn":

* Simple Earn

The simplest type of Earn. **Staking**.

It involves, as you may already know, keeping a pre-set amount of a Cryptocurrency locked for a certain period of time.

In exchange, you will receive interest. The percentage varies based on the Cryptocurrency you lock and the chosen time period.

You can in fact lock it permanently, without being able to unlock it before the end of the contract, or leave it free. In that case, you will be able to take it back whenever you want.

The first option allows you to receive higher interest, but it is more binding. If instead you don't feel like it and want to be able to withdraw it, you have to accept worse interest rates.

Many are attracted by very high percentages, this is because they do not pay attention to a fundamental factor.

That is the platform on which the staking takes place. For example, on **decentralized platforms**, risks and rewards increase.

* Defi Staking

Here is what I was talking about earlier. Staking on decentralized platforms.

So precisely more risks and more earnings.

* Liquid Farming

**Liquid farming** is slightly different from staking.

In this case, the main goal is to provide liquidity to a pair. Earnings depend a lot on the period, based on current demand.

Percentages could therefore change, even with liquidity inserted.

Staking instead remains stable and secure, even if with lower returns.

* BNB Vault

Still staking. But on the platform's proprietary Cryptocurrency: **BNB**.

## Kraken

"The veteran exchange"

Kraken was born in 2011, not long after the **birth of Bitcoin**.

This makes it one of the platforms with the best reputation on the market, together with Coinbase.

It is considered very secure, and we could define it as a slightly more complex Coinbase.

It is in fact a platform that has similar characteristics, but has a structure and **features** that make it more complex.

Unlike Coinbase, it is also possible to trade Cryptocurrencies.

### Features and Services for which to use Kraken

Let's start with fees.

They are equal to 0.9% for stablecoins and 1.5% for other pairs.

They may seem high compared to Binance (which indeed I recommend if your priority is to lower costs), but they are still less than Coinbase, while having a similar level of **reliability**.

Kraken has a function that few platforms have: the **Demo account**.

Also, it has 24/7 customer support.

It manages to be a middle ground between Binance and Coinbase: fees not too high and an excellent level of security. Without missing **trading functions** required by many users.

### Why not use Kraken: the Cons

One of Kraken's problems is that it is possible to **deposit** only via bank transfer and with crypto. No credit card accepted for deposit.

Which can be quite inconvenient considering two factors:

* the transfer can take several days, especially with the weekend in between
* if you want to transfer crypto you must still have another exchange where to buy them

This forces you to always have funds ready on the platform to **enter the market**.
