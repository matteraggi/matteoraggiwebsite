## How to create a Crypto?

Creating a **Crypto** might seem like a very long and complicated process, especially if you have no experience with **programming**.

I am about to debunk this belief. There is a difference between creating a Cryptocurrency and creating a **Blockchain**.

A Blockchain, in case you didn't know, is the technology on which **Cryptocurrencies** and much more are created.

If you want a more accurate explanation, visit my article on [**Crypto Terminology**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/terminologia-crypto/).

Probably now you are thinking: but to create a crypto, don't you need to build its **blockchain** first?

Well no, because you can use existing Blockchains, already created.

It is possible to create your own Crypto without writing a single line of **code**!

The characteristics of the Blockchain: **decentralization**, **transparency**, and **open source** code, make this process very simple.

These Cryptocurrencies, not created on their own Blockchain but relying on an existing one, are called **Tokens**.

There are two differences from Cryptos based on their own Blockchain like Ethereum or **Bitcoin.**

1.  The first is that they cannot be **mined**.
2.  The second is that you cannot change the **technology** on which the tokens are based because you adopt one already built.

There are many **networks** on which you can create your cryptocurrency, the most famous is the **Ethereum network**.

Below I list others that might interest you:

* EOS
* Tron
* Iota
* Neo
* Cardano

## Does it make sense to create a Cryptocurrency?

Let's be clear: creating a **crypto** without an audience, without a clear project on its possible applications, doesn't make much sense.

Don't think about creating your cryptocurrency in 10 minutes and becoming a **millionaire** shortly after because everyone buys it.

If this is your idea, there is no point in reading **how to create a crypto**.

Then it doesn't mean that if you have a clear project to develop and want to take the matter seriously, you cannot succeed.

Here, for example, are some of the most **capitalized** tokens at the moment:

* Binance Coin (**BNB**)
* Polygon (**MATIC**)
* Uniswap (**UNI**)
* Chainlink (**LINK**)
* Sandbox (**SAND**)

Yes exactly, all these Cryptocurrencies are actually **Tokens** built with the method I will show you shortly.

This is to show you that if you have a clear project, you can succeed.

So let's see how to create a **Crypto**.

## First step: choose a Blockchain

The tutorial on how to create a Crypto must start from the **Blockchain**, an important choice even if not complicated.

To play it safe you can choose one of the two most popular: **Ethereum** or **Binance** (which is a fork of Ethereum).

If you don't know what a **fork** is, check my Guide on [Crypto Terminology](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/terminologia-crypto/).

Otherwise, if you are looking for something different that might be more suitable for your project, you have many options.

For example **Solana** or **Polygon**.

## Create a Token on the Ethereum network

It is a very simple process.
It will be done entirely by a **Token generator** like [this one here](https://www.smartcontracts.tools).

Enter the site and click "Create **ERC20** token".

ERC20 is the most used standard for creation on the **ETH** network, you just need to know this.

Once inside the generator, you need the following requirements:

1.  A **Wallet** (e.g., Metamask)
2.  A little **money** in Ethereum for the creation of the crypto (prices always vary)
3.  The **name** of the Cryptocurrency
4.  The **symbol** of the Cryptocurrency
5.  The **number** of Tokens you want to generate (**Total supply**)

Once these simple five steps are completed, you will have created your Cryptocurrency.

If you don't know how to create a Metamask wallet consult [my guide](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/metamask/). If you use Safari, **Metamask** probably won't work; you'll have to use the extension for Google Chrome.

## Create a token on Binance Smart Chain or Polygon

To create a token on the **BSC** or on **Polygon** the process is identical; from the same site used previously, you can also create tokens for these networks.

In the case of the Binance Smart Chain, the token will be a **BEP20**.

If you want to deepen how to create a Crypto on the BSC, I refer you to the [official **Binance** article](https://academy.binance.com/it/articles/how-to-create-your-own-cryptocurrency), where they use a longer technique but without using external tools.

## Second step: find the utility of your Cryptocurrency

As mentioned earlier, besides simply creating it, it is good to give **meaning** to the project you are creating.

With creation alone, you cannot hope that among the thousands of existing **cryptos**, yours will pop up.

If you want to have more customization on your Crypto you will have to access the code and therefore also the **Smart contract** that regulates it.

This can be done through [**Remix**](https://remix.ethereum.org) which is an online application for releasing Smart Contracts.

Also in this case, great **skills** as a programmer won't be necessary, but it will be enough to understand the code a minimum to go and change some data.

## Third step: Create a whitepaper

**How to create a Crypto** that can be reliable? You will certainly need a good **Whitepaper**.

The Whitepaper for a Cryptocurrency is the **document** that illustrates in minimal detail all its functioning and capabilities.

To understand well what to write in it, I recommend you quickly read that of [Bitcoin](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf) and [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/en/whitepaper/).

## Fourth step: how to buy your cryptocurrency

To purchase your Cryptocurrency you will have to enter a **Decentralized Exchange**.

Centralized Exchanges like **Binance** and **Crypto.com** do not put all existing tokens on sale, only the most established ones.

Binance is one of the platforms with the most cryptocurrencies present, yet it will never upload your newly created crypto, which could also be a **scam**.

To buy on a **DEX** (decentralized exchange) you will need to have funds in a wallet, like Metamask.

You just need to enter the DEX and swap your **Ethereum** with the token you prefer.

If you don't find your **coin** by searching for it as ERC20, you can find the smart contract address through [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io/) and search with that.

## Fifth step: beyond the creation of the Crypto

Besides creating your Cryptocurrency you will need to **advertise** it as much as possible.

Through Social Networks, Influencers, and Google Ads.

In addition, you will need a **Website** on which you will present yourself as the creator of the project and where you will upload your **Whitepaper**.

Presentation is very important; it serves to have a human face to rely on.

People trust much more if the Cryptocurrency has a Website where the **creator** exposes themselves and talks about themselves.

**The Crypto Roadmap.**

Another necessary section is the project Roadmap. It shows that you are serious and that you have a long-term vision.

It reassures that the project will not be abandoned and that there will be **updates** and improvements.

The more complete the site is, the better.

To reach more audience you can open a **Blog** section themed **Cryptocurrencies** where you sponsor your project.
