An [**NFT**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/nft/) is a **Non-Fungible Token**. So a Token that is not interchangeable, because it is unique.

A **Token** can be any **digital object** like a musical track, a photo, or a video, for example.

At the base of **NFTs** is the technology called **Blockchain**, which makes them unique. It is the Blockchain that differentiates **NFTs** from normal photos or videos.

A **Non-Fungible Token** is therefore a **digital certification** that serves to attest to the uniqueness and validity of a specific object it represents.

The difference from other creations of **Blockchain Technology**, like **Cryptocurrencies**, is that they are interchangeable, **NFTs** are not.

We can exchange a **Bitcoin** with a Bitcoin, just as we can exchange it with **Ethereum** or Euros.

An **NFT** cannot be exchanged with any other NFT, not having a precise value. They can be determined as **digital works of art**, not as currencies.

In fact, NFTs have become popular and renowned precisely for this reason. As works to **collect**.

A world in which **billions of Euros** have started to circulate, and perhaps for this reason you wondered if it is possible to become part of it **for free**.

Well, unexpectedly yes, actually you can and it is very simple.

So, **How to create NFTs for free?**

## Creating a Crypto wallet

To trade on an **NFT platform** it is necessary to have a **Crypto Wallet**. This step also does not require any expense.

As you can see it is not complicated to **create a free NFT**, and there are no hidden costs.

You can choose where to create your **Crypto Wallet** based on where you will use it most: Mobile or Desktop.

There are many but the most used are: **Metamask** from Computer and **Trust Wallet** from Mobile.

[**Metamask**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/metamask/) is usually the wallet I recommend, also because these **NFT creation** jobs are done from a Computer.

Furthermore, **Metamask** is also present for Smartphones even if it is used less in this mode.

Creating a **Wallet** is a very simple and fast process.

### Create a Metamask Wallet

Install the **Metamask extension** for Google Chrome.

Once installed enter the extension and click on "**Create a Wallet**".

To create the wallet we must set a **Password**, accept Terms and conditions and click on "**Create**".

A **Backup** **Seed Phrase** will be created which will be useful in case you forget the password. Remember to keep it in a safe place.

There, your **wallet** has been created, now you can synchronize it with **Opensea** by clicking on the wallet icon located at the top right on the platform.

### Create a Trust Wallet

**Trust Wallet** can be connected to Opensea only from **mobile**.

But it is easy to use and its application is very convenient for operating on the move.

To create a **Trust Wallet** install the **app** on your phone.

Enter the app and click "**Create a new Wallet**".

Save or write down somewhere the **Backup phrase** provided to you.

There will be no universal username and password so this **seed phrase** will serve you to access the wallet. In fact, the pin you set will be relative to the **device** not the wallet.

If one day you want to access from another device you will need this **security phrase** again.

## Upload the NFT to Opensea

Let's move on to the final phase, the one in which our simple production will effectively become a **Non-Fungible Token**.

A **code** will be assigned to it, which will identify it. Entering the **Blockchain**, thanks to this coding, all movements of this work will always be known.

It is the same thing that happens with **Cryptocurrencies**, a public ledger of **transactions** is kept.

In this case, it serves to always understand if an object is original. Always thanks to this technology you will be able to receive so-called **Royalties** from the resale of a specific object.

If you buy a car today and resell it in 6 years, the resale profit will go all into your pockets. And nothing to the car manufacturer.

With **NFTs** it is different. When you sell a work, you receive forever a **percentage** on every resale. You can choose the percentage within certain limits.

That said, uploading an **NFT** to a platform is not a complicated procedure even if it is divided into several parts.

First of all the choice of the platform. This Tutorial will cover **Opensea** which is the most widespread platform.

Choose whether to upload a **collection** or a **single NFT**. If you want to upload a collection you will have to create one first.

To do this, move the cursor over your profile image at the top right and click on "**My Collection**" in the dropdown menu.

Then, by clicking "**Create a Collection**" you will enter the actual editor.

Here you can choose:

- Logo, Image, and Banner
- Name and Description
- Categories
- Social Links
- **Blockchain** and Payment **Token**
- Graphics with which they are shown

I obviously recommend creating **social pages** where to post content, it is a fundamental requirement to **create successful NFTs**. A good number of followers can show the seriousness of a project.

The choice of the **Blockchain** is important. Personally, I used **Polygon**, very fast and without any cost. Otherwise, you can also use **Ethereum**.

Once the collection is created you can add as many **Items** as you want. Entering the collection and clicking on the three horizontal dots you can click on "**Add Item**".

From this part on there is no difference between those who have a collection and those with a single **NFT**.

Those who have only one **NFT** can access the **Item** creation screen from the dropdown menu, clicking "**Create**".

Once on the page, you can upload **NFTs**, but **one at a time**.

The main part of the upload is obviously the one concerning the **work**, which must have a maximum size of **100 MB.** It can be an **image**, a video, a gif, a 3d model, or an audio file.

After uploading it you can choose a **name** and customize its **link**.

You can write a short **description** for it and create interesting **characteristics**.

Useful especially with a collection, to differentiate **NFTs** from each other, there is the possibility of giving properties, levels, or statistics to the work.

There is also the possibility of including **secret unlockable content** with the purchase.

After that, you just have to choose the **quantity of NFTs** to provide (usually 1).

Here is that your digital creation has become an **NFT**.
