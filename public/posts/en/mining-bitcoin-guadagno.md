## Let's discuss a moment...

## 1) You can earn with Mining

Well come on, we all kind of know this...

Otherwise, who would do it for free?

The Bitcoin network system was created specifically to provide a "win-win" situation.

A win-win situation is one that occurs when both parties benefit from a certain action.

Basically, Miners are happy with the money earned and the network is happy because transactions continue to be verified.

A solid and functioning system, without conflicts of interest.

Where were we?

Ah yes, it is possible to earn with Mining (still today).

## 2) There are places more suitable for Mining

Unfortunately, that's how it is.

And I say unfortunately because one of the two determining factors is against us.

These two factors to keep in mind are:

- temperature
- electricity cost

Well, maybe both are against us.

But let's start with the first one.

The computing power we offer is very high. So high that it puts the processor (or computer, whatever you want to call it) under great stress.

What happens when a computer has to make a great (computational) effort?

To help you answer, I'll ask the question another way:

What happens if you play GTA on your computer for 3 hours straight?

The computer heats up, at the very least.

With GTA open, even 5 minutes are enough to heat it up.

=> The lower the external temperature, the easier it will be for the computer to maintain humane conditions.

For this reason, the place used for Mining is often the cellar/basement. (And probably also because it is not pleasant to have a Bitcoin Mine in the kitchen, but details).

It is no coincidence that the largest and most efficient mines are often underground and in cold places like Russia.

## 3) Mining is increasingly centralized

I'll show you a graph immediately that clearly describes the current situation.

(https://medium.com/@novaminingita/scenario-della-centralizzazione-del-mining-perchè-la-decentralizzazione-è-fondamentale-6484a9420784)

As you can see, more than 80% of Bitcoin Mining is in the hands of only 7 names.

And there's more: BTC.com and AntPool are actually both owned by Bitmain. Which therefore owns almost 40% of the entire world power.

This is certainly not the decentralization you expected, right?

Now calm down. I'm not saying that Bitcoin is a centralized currency. But it certainly isn't as decentralized as one might think.

Despite this, we are not talking about single companies that own all their computing power. But about Mining Pools, therefore services that users use to Mine without having to incur all the otherwise necessary costs.

Basically, the company owns the processors but they are purchased with the money of the people participating in the Pool. And consequently, profits are distributed.

But we'll talk about it better later.

## So Mining yes or no? And how much do you earn?

I admit the decision is complex.

To really understand if it's worth it, it is necessary to make an accurate analysis of costs and possible profits. And these change a lot depending on point 2: the location.

This makes a substantial difference. So what I advise you to do is to understand well what the earnings could be and subsequently calculate the expenses.

Expenses that divide into:

- initial costs for processors
- monthly electricity costs
- maintenance / replacement of processors

Remember that there are no true passive incomes. And that even mining requires some attention.

At the very least a check and a bit of maintenance, even if not very frequent.

## The secondary option

There is another option that could slightly mitigate earnings but eliminate the cons we were talking about entirely.

This option is Cloud Mining.

With Cloud Mining instead of having to:

- create a zone dedicated to mining
- buy processors
- pay electricity bills every month

we go to borrow computing power.

Companies (which are usually located in those very advantageous areas I was talking about) make their processors available.

It was what I was talking about before: Mining Pool. Multiple miners sharing resources to obtain greater earnings.

Basically what happens is this:

- we put our money on a Cloud Mining platform
- we receive interest

Much simpler.
