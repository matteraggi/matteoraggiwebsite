## What is the Internet of Things

The **Internet of Things** (IoT) is the ecosystem that unites the **real** world with the **virtual** one, connecting physical objects in a Web space.

The objects we are talking about are not only mobile phones or computers, but all **Smart Objects**, as objects capable of interconnecting on the Web are called.

This process characterizes [**Web 3.0**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/linternet-delle-cose/), the latest evolution of the Internet that we are currently experiencing.

To give some examples, a smart desk light is a light that can be **controlled remotely** via **mobile phone** or a **voice assistant**. The latter is also part of Smart Objects.

The process we are experiencing lately is precisely a process of **digitization** or **interconnection** of all the objects we use most often. Even objects that until recently no one would have ever thought could become Smart.

The convenience of having many Smart objects is precisely that they can be **controlled from a single device** (our smartphone). Greatly simplifying everyday life.

An even more convenient solution is to control them all through a voice assistant, in this way we won't have to lift a finger because it will do everything.

The process implemented by the **Internet of Things** became public very quickly precisely because its purpose is to **simplify people's lives**.

Obviously, the **technological expansion** of the Internet of Things is not finished but in the future functions will be reached that will simplify our lives even more, as we will see in a paragraph later.

## The characteristics of a Smart Object

* Identification
* Localization
* Status diagnosis
* Interaction
* Data processing
* Connection

## Real-life applications

### Home

The main field for common users is that of the **Smart Home**. In many areas **IoT** has managed to simplify our lives.

The first example that comes to mind is the use of the **virtual assistant** to communicate with all the remaining Smart Objects, which can be: lights, televisions, cameras, power outlets, doors, fans, air conditioning, appliances, and many others.

If all these objects did not have the ability to **communicate with each other** it would not be possible to control them remotely with a mobile phone or voice assistant.

It is possible to control many things even if you are away from home: for example, starting the robot vacuum cleaner to clean the house when we are out or checking security cameras wherever we are.

These technologies influence our daily lives and allow us to **save a lot of time**.

### Industrial / Work Sector

Factories are adopting machines that manage to **interconnect** with each other and with operators. This improves production logistics and **simplifies** the work of plant management.

Even in Agriculture, **Smart** machinery is now used, both to increase productivity and quality, but also to reduce environmental impact.

### Cars

Today every new car produced features **Smart functions**: such as the ability to communicate road conditions or other useful information to the driver.

Vehicles can also connect with many **infrastructures** or with each other.

Lately, technologies are starting to emerge that allow cars to **drive themselves**, they will use 100% of the smart functions available to them and it will also be for this reason that they will manage to drive safely.

## Internet of Things and Web 3.0

The **Internet of Things**, being part of [**Web 3.0**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/web-3-0/), is also connected to other technologies that are developing, such as: **Big Data**, **Artificial Intelligence**, and **Blockchain**.

Many startups are springing up lately in these areas, and from this, we understand at what stage of the transition process from Web 2.0 to Web 3.0 we are.

### Big Data

Smart Objects represent a huge opportunity for the world of **Big Data**. They can collect a huge amount of **information** never experienced before, and moreover, it will also be information of **higher quality**.

Information in the 21st century is among the most important values because it allows large companies to open new sources of **profit**. Not for nothing, often the information extrapolated from users is **paid for**.

They can serve to:

* Create new products or services
* Optimize existing products and services
* Sell the data itself

### Artificial Intelligence

**Artificial Intelligence** is certainly connected to IoT, as it is with any new technology.

Every Smart Object must be equipped with its own **AI** and if the capabilities of these objects increase over time it is also thanks to the technological advancement of Artificial Intelligence.

We are in the era of Digital Intelligence and for some years now there has been an attempt to make every artificial object **smarter**.

If Smart Objects exist it is because we have managed to create an intelligent AI.

### Blockchain

When talking about virtual/digital objects, one cannot fail to talk about the **Blockchain**.

If you are interested in knowing other terms about the world of cryptocurrencies, you can consult my article [Crypto Terminology](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/terminologia-crypto/).

This technology is capable of allowing very high **security** on the network. Thanks to it, it will be possible to have the certainty of a **virtual possession**.

In this historical period in which everything is moving online, it is essential to have an accurate security system there too.

The Blockchain is a **public** protocol, in this way anyone can see who owns a digital object, but at the same time our identity is protected to guarantee us **privacy**.

Smart objects, like many other **digital data**, need advanced protection, and the Blockchain provides it.

## Beyond what we already know

Trying to go beyond the **innovations** that are already finding practical solutions, we can imagine and speculate on what will be possible in a few years.

The **connection** between the Web and the real world will increase even more and reach potentials we cannot even imagine.

In the future, through evolved **3D printing** technology, we could create simple and commonly used objects in a few minutes and at very low costs, simply by **downloading the project** from the Internet.

A common but very interesting and achievable thought is that of a domestic **robot** that can understand what we ask it to do and execute.

It would be the evolution of current Voice Assistants, but with a **physical body** and therefore the ability to perform many more tasks.

The limit to imagination literally does not exist, but a few decades ago no one would have ever imagined **evolutions** like those we are experiencing today, and surely it will be so for us too.
