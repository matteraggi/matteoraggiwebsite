## What are NFTs

NFTs are **Non-Fungible-Tokens**, which means they are not interchangeable with each other.

It is as if they were stickers: you **cannot exchange** different stickers with each other. So unlike **FIAT** currencies and **Cryptocurrencies**, they are **unique**.

They can be used for numerous purposes because their authenticity verification system (based on Blockchain) makes them **very secure**.

A level of **security** that had never been reached online before. Thanks to **NFTs** we can create **digital assets** even more reliable than physical ones.

Before this invention, **digital assets** were considered easy to model and shape.

A photo of an ID card or a photo of a degree has zero value. This is because as you well know it is not complicated to modify them through programs like **Photoshop**.

**Physical assets**, on the other hand, have always been more difficult to **counterfeit**: it was much easier to get a photo of a fake degree than a forgery of the degree document itself.

With the creation of **NFTs**, this changes.

We have the possibility to create **verified digital assets**. Thanks to the **Blockchain**, every virtual document will be verified through codes, therefore impossible to falsify or copy.

This makes them **more secure** than physical ones.

In addition to the possibility of digitizing many other areas that previously would not have had this possibility, **NFTs** also open the doors to infinite **creative possibilities**.

Like the **metaverse**, but we'll get there.

An **NFT** can be a concert ticket, a piece of music, or a work of art.

The problem of the **authenticity of online possessions** can be solved with the use of this technology.

Through an **NFT**, I can have the **certificate of ownership** of my house. And I could continue **indefinitely** with examples of this type.

To sell and buy a home you have to undergo uncomfortable and long procedures, while with **NFTs** a buying and selling operation on the **digital property** would suffice.

The **decentralized system** of the **Blockchain** makes us 100% sure that this operation cannot be fake.

## What is the Metaverse in short

Actually, very little is known about the **Metaverse**. After the announcement of **Meta** (formerly Facebook), people started talking about it a lot, but some existed even before.

Although **Meta's** idea, the most immersive and interesting one, is to access it with a **headset**, it is not said that it will be so.

**But what is the Metaverse?**

Actually, the **definition** we can give today is the following: a **virtual world** **parallel** to the real world, in which we can carry out exactly the same **activities**, both work and leisure.

In fact, for now, we cannot know how this **technology** will develop, maybe contact lenses will be used.
For now, there are many virtual worlds that are accessed normally from a PC, without a VR headset, such as [**The Sandbox**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/the-sandbox/) or [**Decentraland**](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/decentraland/).

It is also possible that this Metaverse becomes, instead of a **parallel world**, an **integration** with ours, making it "digitally interactive".

But in any case, one thing is certain, and that is that **NFTs** will be used for all our possessions in this virtual world.

Precisely for this reason, I say that **NFTs** are the building blocks of the **Metaverse**: without possessions, even in a virtual world, there cannot be a functioning society.

And the **security of NFTs** allows us to have a virtual world with functioning identical to the real one. There is money (**Cryptocurrencies**) and there are goods (**NFTs**).

## Metaverse and NFTs are the future

Yes, I know it's a strong statement but wait, let me explain.

It is very likely that in 20 years we will **NOT live** in a parallel virtual world instead of on Earth and I hope so. But despite this, the **Metaverse** could still be a **success**.

You don't necessarily have to interpret it as a **substitute for our normal life**, it can also be something else.

Now I'll explain what I mean.

Some parts of our life that we already spend online on our mobile phones could be replaced by an **augmented reality headset**, much more immersive and maybe much more fun.

Instead of making a **video call** with your girlfriend when you are apart, you can use the **headset** and meet there, having for a few minutes the impression of being together.

Otherwise, if you work from home, instead of holding business meetings on **Google Meet** or **Microsoft Teams**, you can do them in the **virtual world**, same concept but brought even closer to a real experience.
But even just the world of **video games** will open a huge market for **virtual reality** when technology allows for better **graphics**. And **NFTs** play a huge role in this case, with the birth of [**play to earn**](https://morethantech.it/pubblicazioni/554_play-to-earn-cos-e-e-come-funziona) video games, which I will talk about in a future article.

I just anticipate that they could create a **market** so large as to encompass all entertainment, including cinema and TV series.

I could go on forever with examples, but I think the concept is already understood: most likely this **technology** will not replace our life in the **real world**, but the time we already spent on **electronic devices will be** even more immersive.

And what if the **Metaverse** were **augmented reality**?

Instead of using **google maps** to find the right road we could see it illuminated with our own eyes. Instead of searching for information with the **phone**, we could think of the question and find the answer without even typing.

There are many possibilities, so when talking about the **Metaverse** don't just think of **8 billion people** sitting on a couch with a **headset** on their head.
