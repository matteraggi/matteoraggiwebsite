## So how to invest in crypto as a minor?

I start by saying that **Cryptocurrencies** are free and available to everyone. Like any other currency, they can be used by anyone.

In fact, they were created precisely for this purpose, to be **decentralized** and therefore **free** from any law.

The problem is the use of [Exchanges](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/quale-exchange/), where **Cryptocurrencies** are purchased.

They are usually forbidden to minors, as are almost all **banking services**.
This makes access very difficult for those who have not yet reached the age of majority.

But there are still ways to do it, even if often complicated.

The main options are these:

* **Your parents' data**
* **Bitcoin ATMs**
* **Exchanges that do not require documents (No-KYC)**
* **Mining**

Let's look at the best methods among these for investing in **crypto** as a minor.

## Using a Bitcoin ATM

I am sure that when wondering **how to invest in crypto as a minor** this is not the first thought.

Because almost everyone believes that they do not exist in Italy.
I thought so too but it is not so.

If you live in a city of at least medium size you will be able to find at least one.

The main problem in this case is finding one that does not ask for **identity verification**.

Some ask for it, others do not.

Once found, you can proceed with the purchase directly from the **ATM** by following the procedure I am about to explain.



### How to use a Bitcoin ATM

If you find a **Bitcoin ATM** where identity verification is not required, the procedure is very simple.

**1) Choose the Cryptocurrency you want to buy**

Also in this case it depends on the type of **ATM**.

Some have only **Bitcoin** others allow you to buy more Cryptocurrencies.

If you want to buy Bitcoin obviously you won't have any kind of problem, for other **Cryptocurrencies** instead you will have to be luckier.

You will not find all the Cryptocurrencies that exist in **Exchanges**, but it is a small compromise that can be accepted.

**2) Scan your [wallet](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/dove-conservare-criptovalute/) via a QR code**

The ATM will have a reader for QR codes to allow you to connect it and receive the **Crypto**.

Otherwise, you can enter your Wallet address by typing it on the keypad.

If you do not own a **crypto wallet** do not worry, you do not need to be of age and I explain how to create one [right here](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/metamask/).

**3) Insert cash and confirm the operation**

In some ATMs phases 2 and 3 are inverted but the **process** remains the same.

If there are ATMs where identity verification is required, you can momentarily borrow the **document** from some adult friend of yours.

The difference is that using the document in an ATM, like if you use it to enter a club, your friend will not have any kind of repercussion.

While using it in an **Exchange** every **operation** will be in their name and this is something that absolutely cannot be done.

The weak point of this technique is that you will not be able to do **trading**.

### How to get a credit card as a minor

You will need a **credit/debit card**. However, there are cards that, with parental consent, you can get even as a minor.

In reality, some schools also provide, usually in collaboration with the post office, **student credit cards**.

If they gave it to you, you can use that directly, loading it with cash at a **PosteItaliane** ATM point.

If you don't have this "Student Card", don't worry, there are other **banks** that provide these services.

Here are some listed:

* **Hype**

To create a Hype account if you are under 18, you must follow the same procedure as creating a normal account.

The only difference is that before opening, an email will be sent to parents.

They will have to give **consent** to opening the account.

* **Yap, Revolut Junior, Flowe Junior**

All these apps work great, only that to open an account for minors the parents need to be customers.

If your parents do not have an account with one of these services, you should use **Hype**.

Most likely they will use other banks, because all these online accounts were born relatively recently.
