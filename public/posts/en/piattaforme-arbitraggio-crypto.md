## What is Arbitrage?

Arbitrage is not a **Crypto** exclusive. But it has always been done on the **Forex** market.

The Forex market is the **currency exchange** market. Where Trading is done on pairs like EUR/USD (so euro-dollar).

Arbitrage can be applied to any **market**.

Now I'll explain briefly what it is.

Arbitrage consists of profiting from **price differences** of the same Asset.

Are you wondering how an **Asset** can have different prices?

It happens more often than you might think.

These differences can occur in several ways:

1. On different **platforms**
2. In a **triangular** way
3. By geographical **position**

And these are the three ways in which arbitrage can be done.

Here is an example of the first type (platform arbitrage).

1 Bitcoin equals $30005 on **Binance**. But $30010 on Coinbase.

(In case you don't know what Binance and Coinbase are, I refer you to my [Guide on the best Exchanges](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/migliori-exchange-crypto/))

Returning to the example ... I buy on Binance at a lower price and sell on Coinbase at a higher price.

In this way, I generate a **profit** practically out of nothing.

These small differences are impossible to control. And it will always be like this because the **market** is very large and volatile.

Obviously:

- the gains for a single **Trade** are not high
- they are **risk-free** operations
- it is very **tiring** to do it manually

Now you understand what **Market Arbitrage** is.

But there is a big problem that plagues it.

**Price** differences are minimal and rapid. And prices vary continuously.

It is complicated to operate so **quickly**.

The solution to this problem are **platforms** that do this work for you.

These are mainly **automatic Bot** services which are therefore much more reactive than a human being.

The arrival of these automation tools makes Arbitrage a **semi-passive income** possibility.

## The 3 types of Crypto Arbitrage

I already gave you an example of the first one. You buy on one platform and sell on another.

That is the **classic** one, the most frequent, simple, and used.

Then there are the other two.

**Triangular Arbitrage** and **position** arbitrage.

Triangular consists of a series of operations on **a single platform**.

An image produced by [Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/activity/referral-entry/CPA?ref=CPA_00UL1P5UZR) explains perfectly what it is about.

![crypto arbitrage binance](/blog/arbitraggio-crypto-binance.png)

**Three consecutive trades**, exploiting price discrepancies on the single platform, lead to a **profit**.

**Position** arbitrage instead concerns the state from which you buy.

In some cases, **Asset** prices change from nation to nation.

Using a **VPN**, these value differences can also be exploited.

Here is an example of position arbitrage.

I buy a Bitcoin connecting from a platform in **Thailand**. And simultaneously I sell a [Bitcoin](https://www.matteoraggiblog.com/investire-in-bitcoin/) connecting from Italy.
